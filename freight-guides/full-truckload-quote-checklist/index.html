Full Truckload Quote Checklist for Shippers

Prepare a complete full truckload request with lane, equipment, commodity, timing and facility details.

A full truckload quote is easier to evaluate when the request describes the freight and the operating conditions—not just the pickup and delivery cities. A complete request helps the provider identify the right equipment and avoid preventable changes after dispatch.

Describe the load

  • Commodity and any handling or regulatory considerations
  • Total weight and approximate dimensions
  • Pallet count, floor-loaded status and loading sequence
  • Securement, temperature, tarping or other equipment requirements

Specify the equipment

  • Dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck or another equipment type as applicable
  • Any trailer length, door, height or specialized requirements
  • Whether the load is over-dimensional or overweight
  • Whether driver assist, loading or unloading services are required

Confirm lane and schedule

  • Exact pickup and delivery addresses
  • Ready time, appointment windows and facility hours
  • Required delivery date and any hard cutoff
  • Stops, driver instructions and site restrictions

Check every quote line and operating assumption

  • Linehaul, fuel surcharge and separately listed fees.
  • Equipment type, trailer requirements and loading or securement expectations.
  • Detention, layover, stop-off, tarp, driver-assist and cancellation terms.
  • Liability, cargo coverage options, exclusions and claims process.
  • Pickup and delivery appointments and written booking acceptance.

Compare the commercial terms

  • Linehaul price and included services
  • Detention, layover, stop-off, tarp or other accessorial terms
  • Fuel surcharge and any other separately listed charges
  • Cancellation, rescheduling and payment terms

Frequently asked questions

What is essential for a truckload quote?

Lane, commodity, weight, dimensions, equipment type, pickup readiness and delivery requirements.

Can I request a specific trailer type?

Yes. State the equipment requirement and confirm the provider can cover it for the lane and date.

Is a quote a confirmed truck?

A quote is not a confirmed dispatch. Confirm booking acceptance, equipment and schedule before relying on the move.
