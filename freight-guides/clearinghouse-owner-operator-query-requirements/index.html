FMCSA Clearinghouse Requirements for Owner-Operators

A single-driver owner-operator can have both employer and driver responsibilities under the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse rules. Treat registration, C/TPA designation, query consent and annual checks as separate steps.

Important scope note.

The Clearinghouse rules apply to covered CDL drivers and employers. Confirm your role and the current FMCSA instructions before conducting or delegating a query.

Understand the two roles

FMCSA states that an owner-operator who employs themself as a CDL driver is subject to requirements that apply to both employers and drivers. That means the owner-operator must address employer-side query and reporting duties as well as the driver's own obligations.

Designate a consortium or third-party administrator

An owner-operator must designate a consortium/third-party administrator (C/TPA) to perform the employer-side functions assigned to it. Complete the designation in the Clearinghouse before expecting the C/TPA to act on the carrier's behalf, and confirm who is responsible for each required step.

Plan pre-employment and annual queries

Employers must query the Clearinghouse for covered CDL drivers before allowing them to perform safety-sensitive functions and at least once each year for current employees. Owner-operators must include themselves when they are the covered driver. A designated C/TPA may conduct queries on the owner's behalf under the required consent and authorization.

Obtain the correct consent

FMCSA distinguishes limited and full queries. A limited query requires general consent obtained outside the Clearinghouse; a full query requires the driver's specific electronic consent in the Clearinghouse. Keep the consent process aligned with the query type and do not assume one consent covers every situation.

Keep the responsibility visible

Maintain a simple record of the query schedule, driver coverage, C/TPA designation and unresolved consent requests. The Clearinghouse retains query history, but the employer remains responsible for making sure required queries and reporting are completed.
