Freight Carrier Broker Setup Packet Checklist

A carrier packet is an onboarding file, not one universal government form. Each broker can set its own document, insurance, portal and payment requirements, so confirm the current checklist with the broker before sending sensitive information.

Important scope note.

Verify the broker and the requested submission channel before sharing tax, banking or insurance documents. Do not send passwords or sensitive account credentials.

Confirm the carrier's legal identity

Prepare the carrier's exact legal name, business address, USDOT and MC identifiers where applicable, authorized contact information and a current W-9. Keep names and addresses consistent across the packet and the carrier's public registration records.

Check authority and insurance requirements

Use FMCSA resources to review the broker or carrier record as appropriate. Provide a current certificate of insurance through the broker's approved channel, and confirm any required limits, endorsements or additional-insured wording directly with the insurance agent and contracting party.

Document payment and factoring arrangements

Confirm the pay-to entity, remittance contact and payment method. If a factoring company has a notice of assignment or other payment-control document, provide the current paperwork through the broker's secure process and make sure the broker acknowledges the correct remittance instructions.

Describe equipment and operating coverage

Keep a concise equipment list, service area, operating contact, dispatch hours and any shipment restrictions ready. The broker may ask for additional details about commodities, lanes, tracking, appointments or proof-of-delivery handling.

Use the broker's approved portal and retain confirmation

Check file formats, expiration dates, required signatures and the correct upload location. Save the packet submission confirmation and any approval notice. A complete packet does not guarantee that a broker will tender a load or approve a particular lane.

Verify counterparties before accepting a load

Check the broker's identity and authority before relying on an emailed rate confirmation. NorthStar's freight broker checklist can help organize that review; confirm the result through official FMCSA sources.

Keep the next step documented

Save the agreement, source records, filing confirmation or written instructions that apply to the specific load or operation. If the question involves a legal dispute, regulatory interpretation or a material payment claim, consult a qualified professional about the facts.
