FMCSA MCS-150 Biennial Update Checklist

The MCS-150 is the Motor Carrier Identification Report used to update a USDOT record. A carrier should treat the biennial update as a recurring compliance task and correct material company information when it changes.

Important scope note.

Do not rely on an old snapshot or a third-party reminder alone. Confirm the current filing status and instructions through FMCSA.

Confirm whether your operation must file

FMCSA requires covered motor carriers and other USDOT registrants to keep their registration information current. The biennial update is a minimum recurring requirement; an update may also be needed when company information changes before the next scheduled filing.

Review the information before submitting

Compare the legal business name, principal and mailing addresses, contact details, operation classification, cargo categories, power units, drivers and mileage information with current company records. Make sure the person submitting the update is authorized to act for the registrant.

Use the official FMCSA filing route

Use the FMCSA Portal and the current MCS-150 instructions for your record. Avoid paying an unsolicited third party simply to file a routine update; confirm any service provider's role and fee before sharing credentials or business information.

Save proof and verify the public record

Keep the submission confirmation, date, submitted information and any correspondence. After processing, review the public carrier snapshot for the updated information. A submitted form and a refreshed public record are separate steps, so follow up if the record does not reflect the change.

Do not treat this as a substitute for other filings

An MCS-150 update does not replace insurance filings, authority applications, UCR obligations, state registrations or other requirements that may apply to the operation. Review each requirement separately and note jurisdiction-specific deadlines.

FMCSA moved registration actions into Motus in 2026. Before filing an update, review our Motus registration transition guide and confirm the latest official FMCSA notice for your USDOT record.
