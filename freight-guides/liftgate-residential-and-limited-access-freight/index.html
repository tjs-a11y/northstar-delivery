Liftgate, Residential and Limited-Access Freight: What to Confirm

A freight quote is only comparable when the pickup and delivery locations can actually handle the shipment.

Accessorials are services or location conditions beyond a standard dock-to-dock move. A missing requirement can change the service plan, price or whether a carrier can complete the pickup or delivery. Describe both ends of the shipment before comparing quotes.

Start with the facility, not the fee

Ask the shipper and receiver how freight moves from the truck to the building. Confirm whether each location has a usable loading dock, forklift, receiving hours and staff available to unload. A business address does not automatically mean a dock is available.

Common details to disclose

  • Liftgate: Is a dock or forklift unavailable, making a truck-mounted lift platform necessary?
  • Residential: Is either end a home, apartment complex or residential-style location?
  • Limited access: Is the site a school, construction site, farm, military facility, trade show or other location with special entry or delivery procedures?
  • Appointment: Must the driver arrive within a booked time window?
  • Inside or special handling: Is the carrier being asked to move freight beyond the normal delivery point?

Build a complete accessorial checklist

  1. Confirm the exact pickup and delivery addresses and ZIP codes.
  2. Describe the receiving environment and available equipment at each end.
  3. State operating hours, appointment rules and contact numbers.
  4. Identify stairs, long carries, restricted entrances or security check-in.
  5. Ask the provider to list each included service and any additional charge in writing.

Do not assume a quote includes a service simply because the address or notes suggest it.

Ready to compare freight options?

Prepare the shipment details above, then compare service type, accessorials, timing and written booking terms with the provider.

Frequently asked questions

Does every freight delivery need a liftgate?

No. A liftgate is generally relevant when the location cannot load or unload freight from a dock or forklift and the selected truck offers that equipment. Confirm the actual equipment and service with the provider.

Is residential delivery the same as limited access?

Not necessarily. They describe different location or service conditions. Explain the actual site and let the quoting provider classify the requirement.

Can I compare two quotes with different accessorials?

Not fairly. First make the requested pickup and delivery services consistent, then compare the total quoted terms.
