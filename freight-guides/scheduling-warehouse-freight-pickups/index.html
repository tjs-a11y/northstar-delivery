How to Schedule a Freight Pickup at a Warehouse or Distribution Center

A pickup date is not enough: the carrier needs a workable window, access instructions and freight that is actually ready.

Warehouse and distribution-center pickups often depend on receiving rules, dock capacity, appointment systems and the time needed to stage freight. Confirm these details before requesting a quote or accepting a booking.

Confirm readiness and pickup window

  • Ask when the shipment will be fully packed, palletized, labeled and available.
  • Confirm the facility's shipping hours, cutoff times, lunch closures and holiday schedule.
  • Ask whether the facility requires an appointment or uses a first-come dock queue.
  • Provide a realistic ready time rather than an estimated production completion time.

Give the carrier usable site instructions

  • Full pickup address, building or dock number and ZIP code.
  • Shipping office phone number and on-site contact.
  • Check-in, gate, security or driver identification requirements.
  • Trailer restrictions, dock-height limitations or special vehicle requirements.
  • Whether a forklift or other loading equipment is available and who operates it.

Close the loop after booking

  1. Get written confirmation of the pickup date, appointment or window and service.
  2. Make sure the shipment count, dimensions, weight and commodity match the quote.
  3. Have the bill of lading and packing list ready for the driver.
  4. Notify the provider promptly if the freight will not be ready or the facility changes its appointment.
  5. Retain the booking confirmation and pickup receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Does a quoted pickup date guarantee collection?

No. Confirm that the provider has accepted the booking and that the carrier, equipment and pickup details are confirmed.

What if the warehouse only allows appointments?

Disclose that requirement before booking and obtain confirmation of the appointment process and time window.

Who should arrange the dock appointment?

Responsibilities vary by facility and provider. Confirm in writing whether the shipper, carrier or broker must schedule it.
