How to Compare Freight Quotes and Accessorial Charges

Compare freight quotes on a like-for-like basis by checking accessorials, site requirements and the details behind the base rate.

Two freight quotes can look similar while covering different services. The base price is only one part of the decision. Before choosing a provider, compare the shipment assumptions and the charges that may apply at pickup, in transit or at delivery.

Normalize the shipment details

  • Use the same origin and destination for each quote.
  • Keep pallet count, dimensions, total weight and commodity consistent.
  • Use the same equipment type and requested dates.
  • Describe the same pickup and delivery conditions to each provider.

Look for accessorial exposure

  • Liftgate service when a dock or forklift is unavailable
  • Limited-access or residential pickup and delivery
  • Inside delivery, appointment or notification requirements
  • Detention, layover, stop-off or re-delivery charges
  • Tarping, driver assist or special handling where relevant

Ask what is included

  • Does the displayed price include fuel surcharge?
  • Are appointments and accessorials priced or billed separately?
  • What happens if actual dimensions or weight differ?
  • What documentation is required to dispute an adjustment?

Compare the total cost and operating risk

  • Check whether fuel surcharge, minimum charges, stop-off or other line items are included or separate.
  • Review the provider's process for reweighs, reclassification and shipment-detail corrections.
  • Compare transit estimates, handling, liability terms, exclusions and claims procedures.
  • Confirm who must schedule appointments and what happens if the freight or facility is not ready.

Choose based on total fit

  • Compare the expected all-in cost using the same assumptions.
  • Consider transit estimate, handling and facility compatibility.
  • Keep written confirmation of special services and agreed terms.
  • Do not select a quote that omits a service your shipment requires.

Frequently asked questions

What are accessorial charges?

They are additional charges for services or conditions beyond standard freight transportation, such as liftgate, limited access or detention.

Why did the final freight charge differ from the quote?

Differences in actual dimensions, weight, commodity, accessorial services or shipment details can result in adjustments.

Should I choose the lowest base rate?

Compare the total expected cost and service requirements, not just the headline amount.
