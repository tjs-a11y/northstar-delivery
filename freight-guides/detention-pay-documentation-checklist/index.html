Detention Pay Documentation Checklist for Carriers

Detention terms are usually specific to the load agreement. The most useful preparation is a reliable timeline that shows when the truck arrived, checked in, was released and notified dispatch.

Start with the paperwork.

Keep the load number, signed rate confirmation, dispatch messages, delivery records and settlement together. The written agreement and applicable rules control the result.

Check detention and TONU terms →

Read the detention clause before arrival

Find the free-time allowance, start and stop events, rate, cap, required notice method and documentation rules. Do not assume every broker or shipper uses the same clock or pays the same amount.

Capture the timeline at the facility

Record the appointment time, gate arrival, check-in, dock assignment, loading or unloading start, release and exit. Keep gate tickets, signed paperwork, ELD or dispatch timestamps and other records that the agreement accepts.

Notify dispatch using the required channel

Send the arrival or delay notice as soon as the contract requires. Keep the timestamp and acknowledgement. If the delay continues, send updates at the intervals required by the written terms and ask for approval before assuming an amount is authorized.

Submit a complete request

Include the load number, facility, appointment, timeline, free-time calculation, requested amount under the agreed rate and supporting records. Separate the facts from the amount requested so the reviewer can verify both.

Keep the terms in context

Detention is not automatic simply because a truck waited. Contract wording, notice, cause of delay, facility records and applicable law can affect the result. This guide is operational education, not a promise of reimbursement.

Keep the next step documented

Save the source documents, the date you submitted them and any response. If the issue involves a contract dispute, regulatory interpretation or a material payment claim, consult a qualified professional for advice about the specific facts.
