Freight Carrier Invoice Submission Checklist

A complete invoice packet reduces avoidable back-and-forth. Each broker or shipper can have its own portal, document and timing rules, so use the written instructions for the specific load.

Start with the paperwork.

Keep the load number, signed rate confirmation, dispatch messages, delivery records and settlement together. The written agreement and applicable rules control the result.

Reconcile the invoice with the settlement →

Match the invoice to the load

Check the load number, legal carrier name, bill-to entity, delivery date, agreed amount and payment instructions. Make sure the invoice matches the final rate confirmation or written amendment.

Attach delivery proof

Include the signed proof of delivery and any bill of lading pages required by the customer. Preserve exception notes, seal information, appointment records or other documents requested for that commodity or facility.

Add accessorial evidence

Attach approved receipts and records for detention, layover, lumper, extra stops, tolls or other reimbursable charges. Include the written approval and the timestamps or facility documents required by the contract.

Confirm the submission route

Use the broker or shipper's required portal, email address or invoice workflow. Check file format, naming, required fields and whether the invoice must be submitted by the carrier or its factoring company.

Keep proof of submission and reconcile payment

Save the portal confirmation or sent message, then compare the remittance and deposit with the invoice. Record missing documents, rejections, deductions and follow-up dates. Payment timing and dispute rights depend on the agreement and applicable law.

Keep the next step documented

Save the source documents, the date you submitted them and any response. If the issue involves a contract dispute, regulatory interpretation or a material payment claim, consult a qualified professional for advice about the specific facts.
