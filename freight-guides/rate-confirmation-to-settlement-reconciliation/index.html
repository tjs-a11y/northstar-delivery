How to Reconcile a Rate Confirmation With a Carrier Settlement

The rate confirmation records the load terms you accepted. The settlement shows how the payment was calculated. Comparing them line by line helps surface missing additions and unexplained deductions.

Start with the paperwork.

Keep the load number, signed rate confirmation, dispatch messages, delivery records and settlement together. The written agreement and applicable rules control the result.

Create one record for the load

Use the same load number across the rate confirmation, dispatch messages, bill of lading, proof of delivery, invoice and settlement. Record the agreed linehaul and any separately stated fuel surcharge or accessorial schedule.

Compare the original terms with approved changes

Check whether the pickup, delivery, stops, commodity, weight, appointment or service requirements changed. Keep written approvals for revised rates and accessorials. A phone conversation may explain a change, but a dated written record makes the audit easier.

Reconcile earned additions

Compare detention, layover, TONU, extra stops, lumper reimbursement and other accessorials against the written conditions and the evidence required by the agreement. A charge may be payable only after notice, approval or a specified waiting period; read the actual terms.

Reconcile deductions and payment fees

Review chargebacks, claims, advances, quick-pay fees, factoring fees and other deductions separately. Identify the contract or payment instruction for each one. If a factoring notice of assignment applies, confirm the correct payee.

Close the loop

Calculate the expected net, compare it with the remittance and deposit, and record any difference as an exception. NorthStar's settlement tool can help organize the comparison, but it does not decide contractual entitlement or compel payment.

Keep the next step documented

Save the source documents, the date you submitted them and any response. If the issue involves a contract dispute, regulatory interpretation or a material payment claim, consult a qualified professional for advice about the specific facts.
