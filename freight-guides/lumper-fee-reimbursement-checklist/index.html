Lumper Fee Reimbursement Checklist for Carriers

A lumper payment is a real out-of-pocket expense, but reimbursement depends on the load terms and the broker or shipper's documentation process. Capture the proof at the facility.

Start with the paperwork.

Keep the load number, signed rate confirmation, dispatch messages, delivery records and settlement together. The written agreement and applicable rules control the result.

Review accessorial charges →

Check the written instructions first

Look for the lumper or unloading clause, any approval requirement, spending limit, payment method and submission deadline. If the facility requires a payment before unloading, ask dispatch how the charge should be handled.

Keep the original receipt

Capture the facility name, date, amount, load reference and payment method. Make sure the receipt is readable and shows the actual charge. Keep a photo or digital copy before leaving the facility.

Record authorization and context

Save the dispatch approval, broker portal instruction or other written authorization required by the agreement. If the charge differs from the expected amount, explain the reason and preserve any facility notice.

Submit the complete packet

Use the load number in the invoice or portal entry. Attach the receipt and required approval, then retain the upload confirmation or sent email. Avoid combining several loads into one unlabeled receipt bundle.

Check the settlement line

Compare the reimbursed amount with the receipt and the written terms. If the amount is missing or reduced, ask which requirement was not met and keep the response with the load file.

Keep the next step documented

Save the source documents, the date you submitted them and any response. If the issue involves a contract dispute, regulatory interpretation or a material payment claim, consult a qualified professional for advice about the specific facts.
