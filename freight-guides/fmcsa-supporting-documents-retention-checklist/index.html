FMCSA Supporting-Documents Retention Checklist for Motor Carriers

Federal hours-of-service rules include supporting-document requirements for motor carriers and drivers. Use the current regulation and FMCSA guidance for the specific operation; this page is an educational summary.

Start with the paperwork.

Keep the load number, signed rate confirmation, dispatch messages, delivery records and settlement together. The written agreement and applicable rules control the result.

Know the federal baseline

FMCSA guidance for 49 CFR 395.11 says motor carriers retain up to eight supporting documents for each 24-hour period a driver is on duty. When more than eight are retained, the carrier must keep the first and last documents generated in the ordinary course of business, together with the required selection of other records.

Set a submission process

FMCSA guidance says drivers must submit records of duty status and supporting documents to the motor carrier no later than 13 days after receiving them. A practical workflow assigns responsibility for collecting receipts, dispatch records, itineraries and other supporting records before the deadline.

Retain the records for six months

The FMCSA supporting-document guidance states that records of duty status and supporting documents are retained for six months. Confirm the applicable record type, date of receipt and any additional retention obligation that may apply to your business.

Keep the records usable

Supporting documents should allow the carrier to connect the record to the driver and identify date, location and time. Organize by driver and duty day, preserve legible copies and restrict access to people who need the information.

Apply the correct rule to the operation

These points summarize federal HOS supporting-document guidance; they are not a complete compliance program. State rules, intrastate operations, exemptions, special categories and later regulatory changes can affect the analysis. Check the current regulation and obtain qualified compliance advice.
