Freight Quote Checklist for Ecommerce and Small Wholesale Businesses

Before comparing freight quotes, turn the order into a complete shipment specification.

A growing ecommerce or wholesale business may move beyond parcel shipping when orders, replenishment or supplier deliveries become larger. The best service depends on the shipment and lane—not a universal pallet-count rule. Use this checklist to make quotes comparable.

Describe the shipment

  • Origin and destination ZIP codes and full addresses when available.
  • Commodity and whether any special handling or regulatory requirements apply.
  • Pallet or carton count, dimensions, total weight and stackability.
  • Whether freight is palletized, floor-loaded or otherwise packaged.
  • Requested pickup readiness date and delivery window.

Describe both facilities

  • Commercial, residential, warehouse, retail or limited-access location.
  • Dock, forklift and liftgate availability.
  • Appointment requirements, operating hours and receiving restrictions.
  • Inside delivery, sorting, labeling or other services requested.

Compare service options and total cost

Ask whether LTL, volume LTL, partial truckload or full truckload is appropriate for the actual shipment. Compare the quoted service, transit estimate, accessorials, liability terms, claims process and any exclusions. A lower base rate may not represent the lower delivered cost.

For recurring lanes, save the shipment specification and quote history so you can identify changes in rate, service or accessorial assumptions over time.

Frequently asked questions

When should an ecommerce seller compare freight instead of parcel?

Compare options when shipment size, weight, packaging or delivery requirements make a freight service worth evaluating. There is no single universal cutoff; price the actual shipment.

Do I need a loading dock?

Not always, but disclose the equipment available at both ends. A liftgate or other service may be required depending on the truck and facility.

Should I choose the lowest quote?

Compare like-for-like services, accessorials, timing, liability and booking terms—not only the headline price.
