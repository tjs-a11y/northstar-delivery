How to Package and Secure Palletized Freight Before Pickup

Good pallet preparation starts with a stable base, a compact load and clear shipment identification.

Packaging choices depend on the commodity, weight, shape and handling requirements. The goal is to keep the shipment stable and protect it through the handling process expected for the service you book. Follow any product-specific, hazardous-material or carrier packaging requirements that apply.

Inspect the pallet and base

  • Use a pallet in sound condition with no broken deck boards, protruding nails or visibly damaged stringers.
  • Check that the pallet is rated for the shipment's actual weight and dimensions.
  • Keep the load within the pallet footprint where practical; do not leave unstable overhangs.
  • Use a pallet type compatible with the handling equipment and destination.

Build a stable unit load

  • Distribute weight evenly and place heavier items low.
  • Keep cartons aligned and avoid unsupported gaps that allow shifting.
  • Use corner boards, banding, stretch wrap or other suitable restraints for the product and load.
  • Protect exposed edges and fragile surfaces; do not let straps crush the product.
  • Mark any no-stack, keep-dry or orientation requirements clearly, then confirm the carrier can accommodate them.

Label and document before collection

  1. Apply readable labels with shipper, receiver and shipment reference details.
  2. Photograph all sides of the loaded pallet and any visible damage before pickup.
  3. Record pallet count, dimensions and total weight from actual measurements.
  4. Keep the packing list and bill of lading available to the driver.
  5. Tell the carrier about unusual shapes, fragile contents or handling restrictions before tender.

Frequently asked questions

Should I shrink-wrap every pallet?

Not automatically. Select wrapping and restraints based on the product, load stability and transport requirements. Wrapping alone may not secure a heavy or top-heavy load.

Can freight overhang a pallet?

Overhang can expose product to damage and create handling problems. If the product cannot fit within the pallet footprint, disclose exact dimensions and ask the provider how it must be prepared.

Should I photograph the shipment?

Yes. Dated photos before pickup can help document packaging condition and visible damage. Keep them with the shipment records.
