Bill of Lading and Packing List: Preparing Pallet Freight Documents

Accurate shipping documents begin with the same shipment facts used in the quote.

A bill of lading (BOL) and packing list serve different purposes. The BOL records the transportation agreement and shipment information; the packing list helps identify what is inside the shipment. Requirements vary by provider, commodity and transaction, so use the carrier's current instructions.

Prepare the shipment facts once

  • Shipper and consignee legal names, addresses and contact details.
  • Pickup and delivery locations, including dock or receiving instructions.
  • Number of handling units and pallet count.
  • Accurate commodity description, dimensions and total weight.
  • Freight class or other classification information when required for the service.
  • Purchase order, shipment reference or customer number.

Keep the BOL and packing list consistent

Check that the pallet count, package count, description and shipment references agree across the quote, BOL and packing list. If the load changes after the quote, tell the provider before pickup and request corrected documents where needed.

Do not use a vague description such as “freight” when a more specific, accurate commodity description is available. Avoid guessing weights, dimensions or classifications.

Before the driver leaves

  1. Review the documents against the freight physically tendered.
  2. Record visible damage or packaging exceptions accurately.
  3. Make sure the driver and shipper retain the appropriate signed copies.
  4. Keep the final BOL, packing list, quote and pickup record together.
  5. Send any corrections through the provider's documented process.

Frequently asked questions

Is a packing list a replacement for a BOL?

No. They serve different purposes. Follow the carrier's document requirements and keep both when requested.

Can I estimate the weight?

Use a reliable measured or documented weight. Inaccurate shipment details can affect the quote, classification and handling plan.

What if the pallet count changes on pickup day?

Tell the provider before tender and ask whether the quote, booking and documents need to be updated.
