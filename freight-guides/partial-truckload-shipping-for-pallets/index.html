Partial Truckload Shipping for Palletized Freight

What to measure and confirm before requesting a partial truckload quote for palletized freight.

A palletized shipment may be too large for a routine parcel service yet too small to justify a dedicated full trailer. Partial truckload can be one option to compare, provided the shipment profile and lane fit the available capacity.

Measure the loaded freight

  • Record each pallet's length, width and loaded height.
  • Include pallet, wrap, packaging and product in the total weight.
  • Note whether pallets can be stacked or must remain upright and unobstructed.
  • Identify unusually shaped, overhanging or fragile pieces.

Check loading and unloading

  • Confirm dock-high access or specify if a liftgate or other equipment is required.
  • Ask whether a forklift is available at both facilities.
  • Provide appointment windows and contact details for both locations.
  • Disclose limited-access facilities, construction sites or other special conditions.

Build a useful quote request

  • Exact origin and destination ZIP codes
  • Pallet count, dimensions and gross weight
  • Commodity and handling restrictions
  • Ready date and delivery deadline
  • Loading, unloading and appointment needs

Compare LTL, volume LTL and partial truckload

Ask whether volume LTL is available for the shipment and how it differs from standard LTL and partial options. Compare the same lane, dimensions, weight, service requirements, accessorials and transit estimate. Do not assume a universal pallet count or guaranteed savings.

Evaluate the response

  • Compare the quote against an LTL alternative where appropriate.
  • Confirm the equipment type and whether the load will share trailer space.
  • Review accessorials and any conditions that could change the final charge.
  • Do not treat an estimated transit time as a guarantee unless the provider confirms it.

Frequently asked questions

How many pallets qualify for partial truckload?

There is no universal pallet-count cutoff. Dimensions, weight, lane, handling needs and available capacity determine fit.

Can partial truckload carry fragile pallets?

Possibly, but disclose the handling requirements and obtain confirmation of the proposed service before booking.

Should I provide pallet dimensions or just total weight?

Provide both. Weight alone does not show how much trailer space the freight occupies.
