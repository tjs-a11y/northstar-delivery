FMCSA Motus Registration System: A Carrier Transition Guide

FMCSA has moved registration actions into Motus: USDOT Registration System. This guide explains what motor carriers, owner-operators and brokers should verify when creating an account, claiming company records or updating registration information.

Start with FMCSA's live instructions.

Use the official Motus portal and FMCSA's Move into Motus page. Do not use a link from an unsolicited email or text.

Open FMCSA Motus guidance

NorthStar is not affiliated with FMCSA and cannot access or change a carrier's registration.

What changed in 2026?

FMCSA introduced Motus as its modernized registration system for motor carriers, brokers, freight forwarders and other regulated entities. FMCSA says all other customers, including new and existing registrants, could create profiles beginning May 19, 2026. The agency also stated that the FMCSA Portal's registration-options functionality would be permanently disabled beginning May 14, 2026 at 8:00 p.m. Eastern.

The FMCSA Portal may remain available for other functions. The important distinction is that registration applications and updates moved to Motus; do not assume an old Portal workflow is still the correct place to file.

What can a registrant do in Motus?

  • Apply for a USDOT Number, operating authority or another applicable registration.
  • Submit a biennial update and update company information.
  • Apply for additional authority or registrations, or request reinstatement where available.
  • Inactivate or reactivate a USDOT Number and review the status of registration actions.
  • Manage authorized users and company access after identity verification.

Available actions depend on the entity type, role and registration record. A company official should confirm who is authorized to act for the business before adding users or submitting changes.

Carrier preparation checklist

  1. Confirm the company official. Identify the person who can verify the business and manage authorized access. Keep control of the email address and identity-verification method used for the account.
  2. Gather current business records. Have the legal business name, physical and mailing addresses, USDOT number, operating-authority details and current contact information available.
  3. Review the existing record first. Compare the information in FMCSA's public record with the company's current documents. Resolve stale addresses, contact details or authority questions using FMCSA's current instructions.
  4. Use the official sign-in route. Follow FMCSA's identity-verification and login instructions from the official Motus site. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or account recovery links with a dispatcher or filing service.
  5. Save confirmation. Keep the submission date, confirmation number, screenshots or downloaded receipts, and any follow-up request from FMCSA.

Important: check the current biennial-update notice

Motus launch and stabilization have affected the timing of some registration actions. FMCSA has published temporary guidance concerning enforcement of biennial updates for certain registrants with due dates on or after June 1, 2026. That is not a blanket exemption from keeping registration information accurate. Before assuming a deadline has moved, read the latest FMCSA notice for the specific USDOT record and check the current status in Motus.

Do not rely on an old reminder, a third-party blog or a general calendar rule when FMCSA has issued a newer notice. The applicable requirement can depend on the entity's registration and the date of its update.

Intrastate and state-specific operations

Federal registration requirements do not replace state requirements. A carrier operating only within one state may still need a USDOT number or other state-level registration depending on that state's rules and the operation. FMCSA's federal system does not determine every intrastate filing obligation. Check the relevant state transportation agency as well as FMCSA.

Keep the next step documented

Save the current registration record, every filing confirmation and any written instruction from FMCSA. If an application is delayed, duplicated or rejected, use the official FMCSA support route and retain the ticket or case reference. Do not submit duplicate applications simply because a status screen has not refreshed.
