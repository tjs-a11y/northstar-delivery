Import Landed Cost & Customs Duty Calculator

Look up your product's duty rate in the official HTS — this tool will not guess a tariff code for you.

How it works: estimated landed cost = goods/customs value + freight + insurance + duty + other fees. Duty = value × the ad valorem HTS rate you enter; other fees are also treated as a percentage of goods value. This simplified estimate does not calculate specific or compound duties, quotas, trade remedies, MPF/HMF caps or other entry-specific charges. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

Check the current U.S. Harmonized Tariff Schedule before entering a rate. CBP guidance on determining duty rates explains that classification is product-specific and the correct rate is determined by CBP. Confirm classification and entry treatment with CBP or a qualified customs broker. This calculator is an estimate, not customs advice or a binding duty determination.
