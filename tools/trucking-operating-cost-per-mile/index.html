How to use it

  1. Fixed costs are what you owe whether the truck rolls or not: truck payment, insurance, permits, ELD, plates, parking, phone.
  2. Variable is per-mile wear: maintenance, tires, and anything else that scales with distance. Fuel is handled separately from diesel price and MPG.
  3. Deadhead matters — empty miles cost money and earn none, so your real break-even is on loaded miles only.
  4. Set a target margin and the tool shows the rate per mile you need to quote to actually keep money.
