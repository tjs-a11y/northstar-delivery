Hot Shot Trucking Payload Calculator

Net cargo is limited by whichever is lower: your combined rating (GCWR) or the trailer rating (GVWR).

How it works: GCWR limit = GCWR − truck − trailer. Trailer limit = trailer GVWR − trailer empty. Your net cargo is the smaller of the two. Axle and tire ratings can lower it further — confirm on a scale.
