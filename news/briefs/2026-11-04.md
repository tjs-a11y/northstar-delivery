# Diesel eases 6 cents as peak inventories back truckload rate push

*Wednesday, November 4, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel slips to $6.292, holiday inventory build supports truckload rate asks, and a lawsuit targets the federal carrier registration system.

**Diesel fell 6.0 cents to $6.292 a gallon this week, still up 9.3 cents over four weeks. Meanwhile, a holiday inventory surge is giving truckload carriers leverage in bid season.**

## Diesel Takes a Small Step Back

- $6.292 per gallon: the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-02.
- Down 6.0 cents (0.9%) week over week, a modest break on per-mile fuel costs.
- Up 9.3 cents over four weeks, so check that fuel surcharges still match your costs.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-02](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Inventory Surge Backs Rate Hikes

- October's Logistics Managers' Index showed a big jump in inventories ahead of the holiday season.
- Some truckload carriers are seeking double-digit rate increases, and some have already entered a new bid season.
- Stronger peak demand gives small carriers a firmer footing when negotiating rates with shippers and brokers.

Source: [FreightWaves: Peak season inventory surge bolsters TL carriers’ double-digit rate ask](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/holiday-inventory-surge-supportive-of-double-digit-plus-tl-contract-rates)

## Lawsuit Targets Carrier Registration

- The Small Business in Transportation Coalition has filed a legal challenge against a federal carrier registration system.
- The system's operators warn that a win could disrupt registration fees for carriers.
- State motor carrier safety funding tied to the system could also be affected, so watch for changes in compliance costs.

Source: [FreightWaves: Trucking group’s lawsuit threatens to upend federal carrier registration system](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-groups-lawsuit-threatens-to-upend-federal-carrier-registration-system)

## Demand Signals Look Mixed

- Manufacturing expanded for a 10th straight month, but survey respondents increasingly cited the Iran war and trade wars.
- Heavier shipment weights lifted ArcBest's LTL tonnage and revenue per shipment, helping it beat Q3 expectations.
- Norfolk Southern and Schneider are partnering to expand East Coast intermodal reach, adding competition for some long-haul lanes.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Manufacturing expands for 10th straight month, but Iran war weighs heavily](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/manufacturing-expands-for-10th-straight-month-but-iran-war-weighs-heavily/873468/) · [FreightWaves: Higher shipment weights help ArcBest beat Q3 expectations](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/higher-shipment-weights-help-arcbest-beat-q3-expectations) · [Supply Chain Dive: Norfolk Southern, Schneider National team to scale East Coast intermodal reach](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/norfolk-southern-schneider-national-team-to-scale-east-coast-intermodal-re/873340/)

## Walmart Bets Big on Automation

- $6B: Walmart's commitment to automate distribution centers with Symbotic technology.
- 25 regional facilities will get the systems, aimed at faster inventory replenishment.
- Faster DC throughput could change appointment timing and dwell for carriers serving Walmart freight.

Source: [FreightWaves: Walmart in $6B Symbiotic deal to automate warehouses](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/walmart-in-6b-symbiotic-deal-to-automate-warehouses) · [Supply Chain Dive: Walmart commits $6B to up distribution center automation with Symbotic](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/walmart-commits-6b-to-up-distribution-center-automation-with-symbotic/873356/)
