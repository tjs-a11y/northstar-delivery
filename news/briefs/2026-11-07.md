# Diesel Eases 6 Cents as Freight Fraud Climbs and FMCSA Enforcement Stays Firm

*Saturday, November 7, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel falls to $6.292, a fraud report ties 40% of stolen loads to compromised communications, and FMCSA's CDL crackdown looks set to last.

**Diesel slipped 6.0 cents to $6.292 a gallon, but it is still up 9.3 cents over four weeks, so fuel budgets stay tight.**

## Diesel Eases, Still Elevated

- $6.292 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-02.
- Down 6.0 cents (0.9%) week over week, offering small relief at the pump.
- Up 9.3 cents over four weeks, so fuel surcharges and quotes still need to reflect higher costs.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-02](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Freight Fraud Keeps Escalating

- 40% of stolen loads in a new Highway report trace back to compromised communications.
- Fraudulent calls jumped 90%, so verify every broker and shipper contact before accepting or releasing a load.
- Carrier ownership reports more than doubled, a warning sign of identity hijacking aimed at small authorities.

Source: [FreightWaves: Compromised communications linked to 40% of stolen loads in Highway fraud report](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/compromised-communications-linked-to-40-of-stolen-loads-in-highway-fraud-report)

## FMCSA Crackdown Expected to Last

- FreightWaves' CEO says large carriers will keep pressure on FMCSA's non-domiciled CDL enforcement regardless of Washington changes.
- He argued non-domiciled CDL holders were probably the main reason the freight downturn dragged on.
- Lasting enforcement could tighten capacity over time, which may support rates for compliant small carriers.

Source: [FreightWaves: Big carriers keep FMCSA’s crackdown alive, Fuller says](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/fuller-big-carriers-fmcsa-crackdown-permanent)

## Rail and Intermodal Shifts

- Schneider National is moving its intermodal business to a new partnership with Norfolk Southern.
- Union Pacific's CEO floated higher rates for shippers opposing the Norfolk Southern merger; the railroad called it an offhand comment.
- Rail network changes can shift drayage demand and lane competition for truckers near intermodal hubs.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Schneider National says rail partner shift offers network boost](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/schneider-national-says-rail-partner-shift-offers-network-boost/873847/) · [Supply Chain Dive: UP CEO’s rate threat adds wrinkle to Norfolk Southern merger process](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/up-ceos-rate-threat-adds-wrinkle-to-norfolk-southern-merger-process/874006/)

## Autonomy and Consolidation Watch

- Aurora's autonomous truck production is delayed by supply and quality issues, though it still targets 1,000-plus trucks by end of 2027.
- PS Logistics bought Tennessee flatbed carrier Roane Transportation Services and its 170-plus drivers.
- Flatbed consolidation in the Southeast could reshape competition for small operators on those lanes.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Aurora’s truck production schedule delayed by supply, quality issues](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/auroras-truck-production-schedule-delayed-by-supply-quality-issues/873995/) · [FreightWaves: PS Logistics expands Tennessee flatbed presence with Roane acquisition](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ps-logistics-expands-tennessee-flatbed-presence-with-roane-acquisition)
