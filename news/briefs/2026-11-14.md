# Appeals Court Overturns NY Funding Denial Over Non-Domiciled CDLs

*Saturday, November 14, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

New York wins a CDL funding fight, AI cargo theft passes $150 million, Mexico tariffs threatened, and LA rail dwell tops nine days.

**A federal appeals court unanimously overturned DOT's denial of funds to New York in a dispute over how long non-domiciled CDLs stay valid.**

## NY Wins Non-Domiciled CDL Fight

- Unanimous: a three-judge Second Circuit panel sided with New York against the Department of Transportation.
- The fight centered on how long a non-domiciled CDL can remain in effect, plus DOT withholding funds from the state.
- Watch for ripple effects on driver licensing rules and on how other states issue these CDLs.

Source: [FreightWaves: Denial of funds to NY over non domiciled CDLs overturned: appellate court](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/denial-of-funds-to-ny-over-non-domiciled-cdls-overturned-appellate-court)

## Fake Carriers Drive Cargo Theft

- More than $150 million in AI equipment was stolen from June 2025 to June 2026, per Overhaul.
- 92% of reported incidents involved fraudulent carrier identities and falsified shipping documents, putting legitimate carriers' identities at risk.
- A suspect in the $100 million Brink's jewelry theft agreed to plead guilty in a related cargo robbery case.
- Court records show the group tracked commercial trucks and hit unattended loads, a reminder to limit unattended stops on high-value freight.

Source: [FreightWaves: AI cargo thefts top $150 million from June 2025 to June 2026](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ai-cargo-thefts-top-150-million-from-june-2025-to-june-2026) · [FreightWaves: Suspect in $100M Brink’s jewelry theft agrees to plead guilty in related cargo robbery case](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/suspect-in-100m-brinks-jewelry-theft-agrees-to-plead-guilty-in-related-cargo-robbery-case)

## Tariff Threats on Mexico Freight

- The president threatened to double tariffs on Mexico immediately unless it halts migrant caravans into the U.S.
- Cross-border carriers could see sudden volume swings if the higher levies take effect.
- Three bills in Congress would significantly restrict presidential tariff power if they become law.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Trump threatens to double Mexico tariffs over migrant caravans](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/trump-threatens-to-double-mexico-tariffs-over-migrant-caravans/874534/) · [Supply Chain Dive: Bipartisan push possible to curb Trump tariffs](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/bipartisan-push-possible-to-curb-trump-tariffs/874347/)

## LA Rail Dwell Tops Nine Days

- Over nine days: rail-bound containers are sitting idle at the Port of Los Angeles, per Executive Director Gene Seroka.
- Slow rail can push more Southern California freight onto trucks and stretch drayage wait times.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Port of Los Angeles grapples with elevated rail dwell times](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/port-of-los-angeles-grapples-with-elevated-rail-dwell-times/874553/)

## USPS Raises Rates, Adds Surcharge

- 5.9% average price increase planned in January for services like USPS Ground Advantage and Priority Mail.
- 11% permanent fuel surcharge is coming alongside the parcel rate hike.
- Higher postal costs may nudge some shippers toward other parcel and final-mile options.

Source: [FreightWaves: USPS to add 11% permanent fuel surcharge along with parcel rate hike](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/usps-to-add-11-permanent-fuel-surcharge-along-with-parcel-rate-hike) · [Supply Chain Dive: USPS readies 2027 rate increase, permanent fuel surcharge](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/usps-readies-2027-rate-increase-permanent-fuel-surcharge/874524/)
