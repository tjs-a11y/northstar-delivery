## Diesel Slides Below $6

- $5.937 per gallon is the national diesel average for the week of 2026-11-16, lowering per-mile fuel cost.
- Down 19.0 cents (3.1%) from the prior week, the biggest relief at the pump in recent weeks.
- Down 33.9 cents over four weeks, which also shrinks fuel surcharge revenue on contract freight.
- Forecasts now point to a 2026 crude surplus, though geopolitical tensions keep risk alive.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-16](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/) · [FreightWaves: Diesel: Are Sky-High Prices Finally Over?](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/diesel-are-sky-high-prices-finally-over)

## Spot Rates Climb Against Fuel

- Dry van spot rates hit a new high even as fuel prices fell, an unusual split that favors spot haulers.
- Despite strong headline numbers, many carriers still find rates disappointing heading into routing-guide season.
- One analyst sees tight capacity driving a longer, steadier rate cycle into 2027, though that is a forecast.

Source: [FreightWaves: Trucking Spot Rates Defy Fuel Costs: An Unexpected Market Move](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-spot-rates-defy-fuel-costs-an-unexpected-market-move) · [FreightWaves: Why Truckload Rates Feel Disappointing Despite Strong Numbers](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/why-truckload-rates-feel-disappointing-despite-strong-numbers) · [FreightWaves: Trucking Economy in 2024: A K-Shape Threatens Consumer Demand](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-economy-in-2024-a-k-shape-threatens-consumer-demand)

## Fraud and Theft Stay Hot

- The ATA is pushing FMCSA to tighten ELD certification so the devices are harder to exploit in freight fraud.
- Organized crews are stealing cargo from slow-moving trains in California, a sign theft rings keep adapting.
- $1.5 million in drugs was intercepted after ABF Freight workers flagged a shipment, and a fraudulent-ID pickup led to four guilty pleas.
- Verify every pickup's identity and paperwork, because bad actors are targeting all modes.

Source: [FreightWaves: ATA spells out short- and long-term solutions to halt ELD fraud](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ata-spells-out-short-and-long-term-solutions-to-halt-eld-fraud) · [FreightWaves: Rail Freight Fraud: Organized Crime’s New Tactic?](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/rail-freight-fraud-organized-crimes-new-tactic) · [FreightWaves: ABF Freight workers uncover $1.5M drug shipment headed to Ohio](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/abf-freight-workers-uncover-1-5m-drug-shipment-headed-to-ohio)

## LA-Long Beach Labor Trouble

- Employers say contract talks with an ILWU local have stalled, raising strike risk at the busiest U.S. container port.
- A disruption could strand drayage capacity and scramble outbound loads from Southern California.
- 5 million container units moved through the Port of LA in five months, its best stretch in 118 years.

Source: [FreightWaves: LA-Long Beach port strike risk grows as employers say ILWU talks stalled](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/la-long-beach-port-strike-risk-grows-as-employers-say-ilwu-talks-stalled) · [FreightWaves: Port of LA Crushes Records: 5M Containers in 5 Months!](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/port-of-la-crushes-records-5m-containers-in-5-months)
