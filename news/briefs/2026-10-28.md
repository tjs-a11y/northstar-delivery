## Diesel Climbs Again

- $6.352 per gallon: the EIA national diesel average for the week of 2026-10-26, eating further into per-mile margins.
- Up 7.6 cents (1.2%) week over week, so check that fuel surcharges on your contracts keep pace.
- Down 3.0 cents over four weeks, meaning last week's jump erased most of the recent relief.
- Fuel and truck stop executives warn limited refining and pipeline capacity could keep diesel elevated into spring 2027.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-26](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/) · [FreightWaves: Diesel prices may stay elevated into spring 2027, fuel executives warn](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/diesel-prices-may-stay-elevated-into-spring-2027-fuel-executives-warn)

## Port Freight Piling Up

- Nearly 35,700 import containers are waiting on on-dock trains at Los Angeles and Long Beach amid reported railcar shortages.
- Record volumes plus slow rail could push more inland moves onto trucks, opening drayage and transload opportunities.
- Port Houston container volumes rose 11% in September on surging imports, a Gulf Coast lane worth watching.

Source: [FreightWaves: Backlog grows as 35,000 containers await rail service at LA–Long Beach](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/backlog-grows-as-35000-containers-await-rail-service-at-la-long-beach) · [FreightWaves: Port Houston container volumes jump 11% as imports surge in September](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/port-houston-container-volumes-jump-11-as-imports-surge-in-september)

## FMCSA Purges Training Providers

- More than 10,000 driver training providers have been removed from FMCSA's registry since December 2025.
- Fleets hiring new CDL holders should confirm a candidate's training provider is still listed.

Source: [FreightWaves: FMCSA removes 10,000-plus driver training providers amid safety push](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/fmcsa-removes-10000-plus-driver-training-providers-amid-safety-push)

## Big Carriers Report Mixed Q3

- Landstar saw strong dry van and flatbed demand from tight capacity and data center buildouts, though insurance claims hurt results.
- Werner expects cost headwinds to linger into the first quarter while it works back to mid-cycle margins.
- RXO founder Brad Jacobs backed C.H. Robinson's acquisition of RXO, citing the need for scale in brokerage.

Source: [FreightWaves: Insurance claims adjustments tank Landstar’s otherwise strong Q3](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/insurance-claims-adjustments-tank-landstars-otherwise-strong-q3) · [FreightWaves: Werner Enterprises sees cost headwinds lingering into Q1](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/werner-enterprises-sees-cost-headwinds-lingering-into-q1) · [FreightWaves: Jacobs, RXO’s creator, backs its acquisition by C.H. Robinson](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/jacobs-rxos-creator-backs-its-acquisition-by-c-h-robinson)

## Cross-Border and Policy Watch

- Mexican trucking groups agreed to four negotiating sessions with officials after a strike disrupted highways in 19 states.
- USTR's pause on China-linked vessel fees is set to lift Nov. 9, but an extension to January is under consideration.
- USPS plans a weekly fuel surcharge in 2027 tied to diesel and jet fuel, matching FedEx and UPS.

Source: [FreightWaves: Mexico truckers reach deal for talks after strike disrupts 19 states](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/mexico-truckers-reach-deal-for-talks-after-strike-disrupts-19-states) · [Supply Chain Dive: USTR weighs extending pause on China-linked vessel fees to January](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/ustr-weighs-extending-pause-on-china-linked-vessel-fees-to-january/872763/) · [Supply Chain Dive: USPS to debut weekly fuel fee in 2027, matching FedEx, UPS practices](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/usps-to-debut-weekly-fuel-fee-in-2027-matching-fedex-ups-practices/872778/)
