# Diesel Falls Below $6 While Truckload Volumes Slip and Rejections Ease

*Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel dropped 19.0 cents to $5.937, truckload volume weakened, and spot rates firmed to $3.56 as rejections hit their lowest since early May.

**The U.S. average retail diesel price fell 19.0 cents to $5.937 per gallon. That relief arrives as truckload demand softens and carriers lose some pricing leverage.**

## Diesel Breaks Below $6

- $5.937 per gallon: the national average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-16.
- Down 19.0 cents (3.1%) from the prior week, a meaningful cut to per-mile fuel cost.
- Down 33.9 cents over four weeks, so the decline is a trend, not a one-week blip.
- Fuel surcharges are pegged to this benchmark, so expect surcharge payments to shrink as prices fall.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-16](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/) · [FreightWaves: Oil market slide brings benchmark diesel under $6 level](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/oil-market-slide-brings-benchmark-diesel-under-6-level)

## Truckload Demand Cools

- 68: SONAR's Pricing Power Index fell as truckload demand weakened, trimming carriers' negotiating leverage.
- 13th percentile: accepted truckload volume gave back its recent gains, so expect fewer loads to choose from.
- Tender rejections eased to their lowest level since early May, a sign capacity is loosening.
- $3.56: the national spot rate still firmed, and broker RXO says the market is not turning deflationary.

Source: [FreightWaves: SONAR Pricing Power Index Falls to 68 as Truckload Demand Weakens](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sonar-pricing-power-index-falls-to-68-as-truckload-demand-weakens) · [FreightWaves: Broker RXO says TL spot rates not entering deflationary phase](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/broker-rxo-says-tl-spot-rates-not-entering-deflationary-phase)

## Amazon Widens Fuel Surcharge

- 3.5%: Amazon's fuel and logistics surcharge will extend to bulk storage and replenishment transportation fees.
- The change takes effect in February, which shows shippers are still under pressure from fuel costs.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Amazon to expand fuel and logistics surcharge to more services](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/amazon-to-expand-fuel-and-logistics-surcharge-to-more-services/874693/)

## Ocean Rates High, Capacity Building

- Ocean container spot rates are still 50% above a year ago, driven by congestion and fuel costs.
- A growing vessel fleet and the return of Red Sea services could bring overcapacity, which would shift import timing for drayage.

Source: [FreightWaves: Red Sea overcapacity looms as ocean container spot rates still 50% above year-ago](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/red-sea-overcapacity-looms-as-ocean-container-spot-rates-still-50-above-year-ago)

## Stolen-Vehicle Ring Busted

- $4.5 million: police recovered 96 stolen vehicles that were headed to West Africa.
- 12 arrests so far and two suspects still wanted, a reminder to keep securing equipment at yards and stops.

Source: [FreightWaves: Police recover $4.5 million in stolen vehicles bound for West Africa, arrest 12](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/police-recover-4-5-million-in-stolen-vehicles-bound-for-west-africa-arrest-12)
