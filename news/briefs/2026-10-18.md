# No Truckload Capacity Rebound Yet as California Diesel Stays Above $8

*Sunday, October 18, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Capacity shows no sign of recovering, California diesel stays high, a border crackdown worries shippers, and fraud cases reach court.

**Despite months of rate inflation, truckload data shows no sign of capacity recovering. Small carriers waiting for a market turn should keep planning around current conditions.**

## Truckload Capacity Still Not Bouncing Back

- Truckload capacity shows no signs of recovering, despite months of rate inflation.
- The rebound many expected is not showing up in the data, so plan cash flow around today's market.
- Intermodal rail volumes rose 10% in the latest U.S. rail traffic count, keeping pressure on long-haul truck freight.

Source: [FreightWaves: No signs of truckload capacity recovering](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/no-signs-of-truckload-capacity-recovering) · [FreightWaves: Intermodal rail better by 10% in latest count](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/intermodal-rail-better-by-10-in-latest-count)

## California Diesel Holds Above $8

- California diesel prices remain above $8 per gallon, and truckers are calling for relief.
- An energy expert warns elevated costs could extend well into next year.
- Carriers running California lanes should price fuel surcharges with high costs in mind.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: California diesel prices remain above $8 per gallon as truckers call for relief](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/california-diesel-prices-remain-above-8-per-gallon-as-truckers-call-for-re/833092/)

## Border Crackdown Raises Disruption Fears

- A U.S. trucking crackdown at the Mexico border is raising concerns about cross-border freight disruptions.
- Carriers and dispatchers on Mexico lanes should watch for schedule and handoff impacts.

Source: [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: US trucking crackdown raises concerns over freight disruptions](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-us-trucking-crackdown-raises-concerns-over-freight-disruptions)

## Courts Hit Verdicts, Scams and Smugglers

- A Texas judge upheld the nuclear verdict against C.H. Robinson, showing large jury awards remain a live risk.
- 32 months in jail: Louisiana staged-accident mastermind Danny Keating has finally been sentenced.
- 25 people were charged after a drug ring used unsuspecting trucking companies to haul cocaine from California to Florida.
- More than 45 kilograms of cocaine were seized, a reminder to vet unfamiliar shippers and loads closely.
