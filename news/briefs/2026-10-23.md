## Diesel Turns Higher Again

- $6.276 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-10-19.
- Up 9.7 cents (1.6%) from the prior week, squeezing margins on loads booked without a fuel surcharge.
- Down 25.3 cents over four weeks, so the weekly jump erases part of the recent relief.
- Check that fuel surcharge tables on current contracts reflect this week's price before quoting new lanes.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-19](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## $12M Theft Ring Used Hijacked Carrier IDs

- At least 24 shipments disappeared after drivers picked up freight at distribution centers, federal authorities say.
- Investigators found fake licenses, altered identities and ransom demands tied to the scheme.
- Hijacked authorities can damage a real carrier's reputation, so monitor your credentials and broker setup records closely.

Source: [FreightWaves: Feds charge driver in alleged $12M cargo theft scheme built on hijacked carrier identities](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/feds-charge-driver-in-alleged-12m-cargo-theft-scheme-built-on-hijacked-carrier-identities)

## Trailer Orders Jump Ahead of Price Hikes

- 385% surge in U.S. trailer orders in September as carriers locked in equipment before possible price increases.
- Class 8 truck purchases sent mixed signals, showing fleets are cautious about power units.
- EPA '27 NOx engine rules are approaching and will affect anyone buying new heavy-duty trucks.

Source: [FreightWaves: US trailer orders soar 385% as Class 8 truck market sends mixed signals](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/us-trailer-orders-soar-385-as-class-8-truck-market-sends-mixed-signals) · [FreightWaves: Ryder fleet facing possible growth need as EPA ‘27 nears](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ryder-fleet-facing-possible-growth-need-as-epa-27-nears)

## Railroads Post Record Quarters

- Union Pacific reported record Q3 revenue and profits as volume and pricing gains outpaced fuel and inflation costs.
- Norfolk Southern also hit record Q3 revenue on higher volumes, rates and fuel surcharges.
- Strong rail pricing and volume suggest intermodal remains a competitor on long-haul lanes.

Source: [FreightWaves: Union Pacific posts record third-quarter revenue and profits](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/union-pacific-posts-record-third-quarter-revenue-and-profits) · [FreightWaves: Norfolk Southern profits rise on higher volume and revenue](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/norfolk-southern-profits-rise-on-higher-volume-and-revenue)

## Port Dwell Times Climb at San Pedro Bay

- Record September volumes at San Pedro Bay ports pushed up cargo dwell times for both rail and truck.
- Longer dwell means more waiting at terminals, so drayage carriers should factor detention into rates.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Extended peak season drives high dwell times at San Pedro Bay ports](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/extended-peak-season-drives-high-dwell-times-at-san-pedro-bay-ports/833584/)
