## Diesel slide keeps going

- $5.937 per gallon: the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-16.
- Down 19.0 cents (3.1%) week over week, a meaningful cut to per-mile fuel costs.
- Down 33.9 cents over four weeks, so revisit fuel surcharge math on spot quotes.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-16](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Cargo theft cases stack up

- $7.2 million theft scheme: a trucker got 30 months in federal prison for his role.
- Prosecutors say that ring used real freight assignments and trucking companies to steal loads in Southern California.
- 14 people charged in a separate $22.5 million ring that allegedly stopped moving freight trains.
- Stolen sneakers, tools and electronics were traced through SoCal storage sites into resale markets; vet partners closely.

Source: [FreightWaves: Trucker sentenced in $7.2 million cargo theft scheme involving legitimate freight contracts](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucker-sentenced-in-7-2-million-cargo-theft-scheme-involving-legitimate-freight-contracts) · [FreightWaves: 14 charged in $22.5 million cargo theft ring that stopped moving freight trains](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/14-charged-in-22-5-million-cargo-theft-ring-that-stopped-moving-freight-trains)

## Port strike threat eases

- Tentative union deal reached at LA-Long Beach, the busiest U.S. container port complex.
- The accord follows more than a year of bargaining, including federally mediated talks.
- Drayage and SoCal carriers face less near-term risk of a sudden freight stoppage.

Source: [FreightWaves: Tentative union deal averts strike at Port of LA-Long Beach](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/tentative-union-deal-averts-strike-at-port-of-la-long-beach)

## Pre-Thanksgiving capacity watch

- Tender rejections typically rise slightly before Thanksgiving, per FreightWaves SONAR data.
- FreightWaves projects rejections could top 14% as low inventories spur last-minute shipping.
- Tighter capacity could give small carriers more leverage on holiday spot loads.

Source: [FreightWaves: Trucking Rejection Rates: Why Holiday Shipping Gets SPICY](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-rejection-rates-why-holiday-shipping-gets-spicy)

## Broker liability case advances

- Split ruling: J.B. Hunt's brokerage won one issue and lost one in Arizona federal court.
- The case is shaping up as a key test after Montgomery vs. Caribe Transport II.
- Outcomes could shift how brokers screen carriers, affecting load access for small fleets.

Source: [FreightWaves: J.B. Hunt broker liability case proceeding after split decision](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/j-b-hunt-broker-liability-case-proceeding-after-split-decision)
