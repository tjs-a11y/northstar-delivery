## Diesel Jumps Again

- $6.352 per gallon: EIA's U.S. average retail diesel for the week of 2026-10-26.
- Up 7.6 cents (1.2%) from the prior week, so make sure fuel surcharges keep pace before booking.
- Down 3.0 cents over four weeks, so the jump erased only part of the recent relief.
- USPS will add a weekly fuel fee in 2027 tied to diesel and jet fuel, matching FedEx and UPS.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-26](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/) · [Supply Chain Dive: USPS to debut weekly fuel fee in 2027, matching FedEx, UPS practices](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/usps-to-debut-weekly-fuel-fee-in-2027-matching-fedex-ups-practices/872778/)

## Mexican Truckers Strike Nationwide

- Nationwide strike began Monday, driven by highway security concerns and rising costs.
- Protests are planned at major freight hubs and could slow commercial and cross-border traffic.
- Border-lane carriers should expect delays and confirm pickup windows with shippers and brokers.

Source: [FreightWaves: Mexican truckers launch national strike over highway security, rising costs](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/mexican-truckers-launch-national-strike-over-highway-security-rising-costs)

## No Carrier-Vetting Rules Coming Soon

- TIA's chief says federal guidance on broker carrier selection is not close.
- Brokers sought it after two court decisions this year went against them.
- Without clear rules, expect brokers to keep tightening safety and insurance checks on small carriers.

Source: [FreightWaves: Federal guidance on carrier selection not around the corner: TIA’s Burroughs](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/federal-guidance-on-carrier-selection-not-around-the-corner-tias-burroughs)

## Motus Repair Still Undated

- FMCSA deputy administrator Jesse Elison again addressed the troubled Motus registration system.
- No date was given for a full repair, so allow extra time for registration tasks.

Source: [FreightWaves: FMCSA’s Elison addresses Motus again, still no date on full repair](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/fmcsas-elison-addresses-motus-again-still-no-date-on-full-repair)

## CSX Courts Truckload Freight

- CSX is reviewing shippers' truckload files to pitch intermodal service and cost comparisons.
- The railroad sees a weak trucking market as an opening, adding pressure on long-haul truckload rates.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: CSX sees rail conversion opportunities from battered trucking market](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/csx-sees-rail-conversion-opportunities-from-battered-trucking-market/833858/)
