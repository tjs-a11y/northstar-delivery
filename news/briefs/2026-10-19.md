# Werner Flags Rising Costs as Truckload Capacity Shows No Recovery

*Monday, October 19, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Werner blames fuel, driver and claims costs for a Q3 miss, capacity stays stuck, and a nuclear verdict against C.H. Robinson stands.

**Werner Enterprises warned Monday that Q3 profit will fall short, pointing to the same fuel, driver and insurance claims costs squeezing small carriers.**

## Werner Issues Q3 Profit Warning

- Werner preannounced weaker third-quarter earnings Monday, a sign that even large fleets are struggling with costs.
- Fuel, driver pay and insurance claims were cited as the drivers, which are the same pressures hitting owner-operators.
- Box truck operator Telly Arnold says newcomers often learn about insurance deposits, claims and breakdowns only after chasing revenue.
- Know your cost per mile before booking loads; thin margins leave little room for surprise expenses.

## Truckload Capacity Still Not Recovering

- Despite months of rate inflation, data shows no sign that truckload capacity is coming back.
- Many in the market expected a rebound by now, so carriers should plan around current conditions rather than a quick turnaround.

Source: [FreightWaves: No signs of truckload capacity recovering](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/no-signs-of-truckload-capacity-recovering)

## Legal Risk Stays High

- A Texas judge affirmed the nuclear verdict against C.H. Robinson, keeping large-verdict risk front and center for insurers.
- Big verdicts that hold up on appeal tend to feed into the claims costs carriers see at renewal.
- A California trucking company owner pleaded guilty in a $2.1M identity theft scheme affecting nearly 100 people.
