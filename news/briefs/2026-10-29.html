Diesel climbs 7.6 cents as Fed holds rates and LTL tonnage turns positive

Diesel rises to $6.352, the Fed holds steady amid inflation, California cargo theft arrests, and Old Dominion sees tonnage growth.

Diesel Pops 7.6 Cents

  • $6.352 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-10-26.
  • Up 7.6 cents (1.2%) week over week, so recheck fuel surcharges on spot loads now.
  • Down 3.0 cents over four weeks, meaning last week's jump erased part of a recent dip.

Source: EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-26

Fed Holds as Inflation Speeds Up

  • Rates unchanged: the Fed kept its main interest rate steady despite accelerating inflation.
  • Two FOMC members dissented, favoring a 0.25 percentage point hike, so borrowing costs could still rise.
  • Truck loans and equipment financing stay pricey for small fleets weighing purchases.

Source: Supply Chain Dive: Fed holds main interest rate steady despite accelerating inflation

California Cargo Theft Crackdown

  • $500,000 SIG Sauer shipment left a Torrance warehouse via a fictitious pickup; two people were arrested.
  • Fake pickups remain a live threat, so verify carrier identity before loading or accepting brokered freight.
  • Nine alleged Sinaloa Cartel associates were arrested in Southern California over a cargo train theft ring.

Source: FreightWaves: $500,000 SIG Sauer shipment stolen in fictitious pickup, two arrested in California · FreightWaves: 9 alleged Mexican Cartel associates arrested in California train theft ring

Freight Demand Signals Mixed

  • Old Dominion posted positive September tonnage, its first growth in more than two years.
  • OD executives say a full recovery needs broader demand, which depends on easing energy costs.
  • XPO beat Q3 expectations on higher volumes and stronger pricing, hinting at firmer LTL rates.
  • CPKC set records for grain and intermodal volumes, adding rail competition for some truck freight.

Source: FreightWaves: Old Dominion’s tonnage turns positive; recovery needs demand inflection · FreightWaves: XPO’s pricing, operating initiatives drive Q3 beat · FreightWaves: CPKC reports record revenue on strength of grain and intermodal traffic

Claims Worries Hit Brokers

  • C.H. Robinson and Landstar shares fell despite strong results, as investors focused on rising claims.
  • Expect brokers to tighten scrutiny of carrier safety and claims history when tendering loads.

Source: FreightWaves: C.H Robinson and Landstar stock prices slammed; insurance a factor
