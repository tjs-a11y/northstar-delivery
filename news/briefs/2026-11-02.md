## Diesel Jumps Again

- $6.352 per gallon is the national diesel average for the week of 2026-10-26, per EIA.
- Up 7.6 cents (1.2%) week over week, enough to erase thin margins on long hauls.
- Down 3.0 cents over four weeks, so the latest jump reverses most of the recent relief.
- Check that your fuel surcharge tables track weekly moves, not monthly averages.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-26](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## SoCal Port Strike Days Away

- A strike at the busiest U.S. port could hit within days as union watchmen and port employers keep negotiating.
- Both sides extended the contract, which buys time but does not settle anything yet.
- Drayage and Southern California outbound carriers should plan for possible container delays and lane disruption.

Source: [FreightWaves: Strike at busiest US port just days away as talks continue](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/strike-at-busiest-us-port-just-days-away-as-talks-continue)

## Factories Keep Growing

- 55.5 was October's ISM manufacturing PMI, beating expectations and marking a 10th straight month of expansion.
- Steady factory output supports industrial and flatbed freight demand heading into year end.
- Survey respondents grew more negative, citing the Iran war, price swings and trade wars as risks.
- Public LTL carriers describe demand with cautious optimism, matching the PMI signal.

Source: [FreightWaves: October’s 55.5 manufacturing PMI beats expectations](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/octobers-55-5-manufacturing-pmi-beats-expectations) · [Supply Chain Dive: Manufacturing expands for 10th straight month, but Iran war weighs heavily](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/manufacturing-expands-for-10th-straight-month-but-iran-war-weighs-heavily/873468/)

## Peak Season Signals

- 4% growth in holiday parcel demand is forecast by ShipMatrix, the strongest peak since 2022.
- Big retailers, including Amazon, reported paying more for freight in the third quarter.
- Rising shipper freight costs plus firmer peak volume may give small carriers more room on rates.

Source: [FreightWaves: Parcel shipping demand to increase 4% during holidays, ShipMatrix says](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/parcel-shipping-demand-to-increase-4-during-holidays-shipmatrix-says) · [FreightWaves: Amazon, retailers paid more for freight in 3Q](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/amazon-retailers-paid-more-for-freight-in-3q)

## Network Shifts to Watch

- Norfolk Southern and Schneider National are partnering to expand East Coast intermodal reach, adding rail competition on some lanes.
- $400M Walmart distribution center planned for Cibolo, Texas, with groundbreaking expected this February.
- The 744,000-plus-square-foot site could create new regional freight in the San Antonio area.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Norfolk Southern, Schneider National team to scale East Coast intermodal reach](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/norfolk-southern-schneider-national-team-to-scale-east-coast-intermodal-re/873340/) · [Supply Chain Dive: Walmart plans $400M Texas distribution center](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/walmart-plans-400m-texas-distribution-center/873218/)
