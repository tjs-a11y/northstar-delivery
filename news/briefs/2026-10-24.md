## Diesel Bounces Back

- $6.276 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-10-19.
- Up 9.7 cents (1.6%) from the prior week, so check that fuel surcharges keep pace.
- Still down 25.3 cents over four weeks, but the weekly jump ends the recent relief.
- The first Russian diesel shipment is headed to the U.S. after a Trump-Putin deal; pump impact is unclear.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-19](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/) · [FreightWaves: Russian diesel tanker heads for U.S. following Trump-Putin deal](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/russian-diesel-tanker-heads-for-u-s-following-trump-putin-deal)

## Costs Hit a Reefer Carrier

- 7% drop in Marten Transport shares followed a weak third-quarter report.
- Rising fuel and health insurance costs hurt results despite better supply-demand balance.
- Small fleets face the same squeeze: tighter markets don't help if expenses outrun rates.

Source: [FreightWaves: Cost pressures sink Marten Transport’s Q3](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/cost-pressures-sink-marten-transports-q3)

## Canada Tariff List Shifts

- Oct. 30: plywood and certain packaging materials come off the 50% Canada tariff list.
- Other goods are being added, so cross-border lane volumes may shift by commodity.
- Wind turbine imports avoid new tariffs for now; officials have 180 days to report on trade talks.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Trump tweaks Canada tariffs, removing certain goods while adding others](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/trump-tweaks-canada-tariffs-removing-certain-goods-while-adding-others/833895/) · [Supply Chain Dive: Trump eschews tariffs for trade talks on wind turbine imports](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/trump-eschews-tariffs-for-trade-talks-on-wind-turbine-imports/833840/)

## LTL Rates and Cross-Border Moves

- 5.9% general rate increase from Roadrunner takes effect Nov. 2.
- The LTL carrier also launched a new Canada-to-U.S. outbound service.
- Shippers facing higher LTL pricing may shift some freight toward partial or truckload options.

Source: [FreightWaves: Roadrunner announces Canada-outbound service](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/roadrunner-announces-canada-outbound-service)

## Freight Demand Signals

- Drewry's World Container Index rose on trans-Pacific and Asia-Europe routes, pointing to firmer import demand.
- More than $1.6 billion in five U.S. plant projects will add trailer, bus, cooling and beverage capacity.
- Ryder stock rose 10.49% to $265.53 on solid Q3 as EPA '27 engine rules near.
