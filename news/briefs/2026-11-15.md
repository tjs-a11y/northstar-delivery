# Diesel falls to $6.127 while holiday spot rates take off early

*Sunday, November 15, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Fuel relief, an early spot-rate run, Mexico tariff threats and a court win for New York on non-domiciled CDLs.

**Diesel fell 16.5 cents to $6.127 a gallon, giving carriers some margin relief just as spot rates climb earlier than usual ahead of the holidays.**

## Diesel eases again

- $6.127 per gallon: U.S. average retail diesel for the week of 2026-11-09.
- Down 16.5 cents (2.6%) week over week, a meaningful cut to per-mile fuel cost.
- Down 5.2 cents over four weeks, so the trend is finally moving in carriers' favor.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-09](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Spot rates move early

- Spot rates are rising ahead of the holidays, earlier than the usual seasonal pattern.
- Small carriers with open capacity may find better-paying loads now rather than waiting for December.
- Pressure remains: truck count is at its lowest since early 2022, with some carriers facing layoffs and bankruptcies.

Source: [FreightWaves: Spot rates jump ahead of holidays](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/spot-rates-jump-ahead-of-holidays) · [FreightWaves: Trucking Recession Continues: Why Carriers Face Layoffs and Bankruptcies](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-recession-continues-why-carriers-face-layoffs-and-bankruptcies)

## Mexico trade: tariffs and traffic

- Trump threatened to double tariffs on Mexico unless migrant caravans are halted, effective immediately if imposed.
- $75.4B: U.S.-Mexico truck freight value in September, so any tariff jump could hit cross-border volume.
- 400 trucks: added Texas cross-border traffic from a Saturday crossing pilot.
- Three bills in Congress would restrict presidential tariff power, though none is law yet.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Trump threatens to double Mexico tariffs over migrant caravans](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/trump-threatens-to-double-mexico-tariffs-over-migrant-caravans/874534/) · [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: Texas cross-border traffic rises by 400 trucks through Saturday pilot](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-texas-cross-border-traffic-rises-by-400-trucks-through-saturday-pilot) · [Supply Chain Dive: Bipartisan push possible to curb Trump tariffs](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/bipartisan-push-possible-to-curb-trump-tariffs/874347/)

## Court sides with NY on CDLs

- A unanimous Second Circuit panel ruled for New York in its fight with DOT over non-domiciled CDLs.
- The case centered on federal funding denied over how long those CDLs can stay valid.
- Watch for ripple effects on driver eligibility and state licensing rules.

Source: [FreightWaves: Denial of funds to NY over non domiciled CDLs overturned: appellate court](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/denial-of-funds-to-ny-over-non-domiciled-cdls-overturned-appellate-court)

## Fraud and port delays

- $150 million-plus in AI equipment stolen from June 2025 to June 2026, per Overhaul.
- 92% of incidents involved fake carrier identities or falsified documents, raising vetting scrutiny for legitimate small carriers.
- Over nine days: rail dwell for containers at the Port of Los Angeles, which can disrupt drayage schedules.

Source: [FreightWaves: AI cargo thefts top $150 million from June 2025 to June 2026](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ai-cargo-thefts-top-150-million-from-june-2025-to-june-2026) · [Supply Chain Dive: Port of Los Angeles grapples with elevated rail dwell times](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/port-of-los-angeles-grapples-with-elevated-rail-dwell-times/874553/)
