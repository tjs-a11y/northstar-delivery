# Diesel Eases 6 Cents as Trucking Jobs Hold Steady and Mexico Trade Hits a Record

*Friday, November 6, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel slips to $6.292, October trucking payrolls stay flat, Mexico cross-border trade sets a record, and container imports climb 9%.

**Diesel fell 6.0 cents to $6.292 a gallon this week, a small break for carriers still paying 9.3 cents more than four weeks ago.**

## Diesel Takes a Small Step Back

- $6.292 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-02.
- Down 6.0 cents (0.9%) from the prior week, a modest break at the pump.
- Up 9.3 cents over four weeks, so fuel surcharges and lane quotes still need a close look.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-02](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Trucking Jobs Stall in October

- October truck transportation employment was unchanged from September, per the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
- September was revised down to 1,471,300 from 1,473,100, a sign the driver pool is not expanding.
- Flat payrolls suggest capacity is holding steady rather than flooding back into the market.

Source: [FreightWaves: October truck transportation jobs = September truck transportation jobs](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/october-truck-transportation-jobs-september-truck-transportation-jobs)

## Mexico Trade Booms, Theft Risk Grows

- $96.3B in September trade set a record and widened Mexico's lead as the top U.S. trading partner.
- Strong cross-border volume keeps freight moving through border lanes for carriers serving Mexico.
- 42% of Q3 cargo theft incidents in Mexico involved parked trucks, according to Overhaul.
- Drivers running into Mexico should treat parking stops as the highest-risk part of the trip.

Source: [FreightWaves: Mexico widens US trade lead with record $96.3B September](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/mexico-widens-us-trade-lead-with-record-96-3b-september) · [FreightWaves: Mexico cargo thieves shift tactics as Q3 truck parking thefts surge](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/mexico-cargo-thieves-shift-tactics-as-q3-truck-parking-thefts-surge)

## Import Freight Climbs as Prices Rise

- 9% rise in North America container imports in September, despite disruptions and higher shipping costs.
- More boxes arriving means more drayage and inland freight for carriers near ports.
- Aurora's autonomous truck production is delayed by supply and quality issues, though it still targets more than 1,000 trucks by end of 2027.

Source: [FreightWaves: North America container imports rise 9% as as freight pricing pressures mount](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/north-america-container-imports-rise-9-as-as-freight-pricing-pressures-mount) · [Supply Chain Dive: Aurora’s truck production schedule delayed by supply, quality issues](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/auroras-truck-production-schedule-delayed-by-supply-quality-issues/873995/)

## Rail Fight Spills Into Public View

- The STB rejected Union Pacific's appeal and ordered release of a video with an alleged rate hike threat.
- The remarks targeted shippers opposed to the Norfolk Southern merger; UP's CEO now says he was joking.
- Schneider National has shifted its intermodal partnership to Norfolk Southern, reshaping rail options shippers weigh against trucks.

Source: [FreightWaves: STB orders Union Pacific to make alleged rate hike threat video public](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/stb-orders-union-pacific-to-make-rate-hike-threat-video-public) · [FreightWaves: EXCLUSIVE: ‘Humor is good,’ Union Pacific CEO says of video rate threat](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/exclusive-humor-is-good-union-pacific-ceo-says-of-video-rate-threat) · [Supply Chain Dive: Schneider National says rail partner shift offers network boost](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/schneider-national-says-rail-partner-shift-offers-network-boost/873847/)
