# Diesel Drops to $5.937 as Truckload Capacity Thins Despite Rate Recovery

*Wednesday, November 18, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel fell 19.0 cents this week, carriers keep exiting despite stronger rates, and the Supreme Court reopens shipper liability questions.

**Diesel fell 19.0 cents to $5.937 per gallon this week, a welcome break on the biggest variable cost for small carriers.**

## Diesel Slides Again

- $5.937 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-16.
- Down 19.0 cents (3.1%) from the prior week, easing per-mile costs heading into late November.
- Down 33.9 cents over four weeks, a sustained drop worth checking against your fuel surcharge schedules.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-16](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Rates Up, Carriers Still Exiting

- Truckload rates climbed much faster than costs in 2026, yet carrier failures continue, according to SONAR data.
- Capacity is still declining as rates recover, a lag that could strengthen carrier leverage in 2027 contract bids.
- Tender rejections are running at more than double last year's level, a sign shippers are struggling to cover freight.

Source: [FreightWaves: The Lag Effect: Trucking Capacity Declines as Rates Recover](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/the-lag-effect-trucking-capacity-declines-as-rates-recover) · [FreightWaves: Carriers can now pass loads they can’t cover to 7 vetted peers](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/graypool-exchange-carriers-pass-uncovered-loads)

## Shipper Liability Back in Play

- The U.S. Supreme Court sent Casarez vs. Irigoyen Farms back to a lower court on Monday via grant, vacate, remand.
- The move could set up a major ruling on whether shippers and receivers share liability after Montgomery.
- Outcome could shape how shippers vet and select carriers, including smaller operators.

Source: [FreightWaves: SCOTUS action revives shipper/receiver liability post-Montgomery](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/scotus-action-revives-shipper-receiver-liability-post-montgomery)

## Enforcement Blitzes Sideline Trucks

- Over 284 out-of-service violations turned up in recent enforcement operations across the U.S. and Canada.
- More than 76 drivers and 208 trucks were placed out of service, a reminder to tighten pre-trip checks and paperwork.

Source: [FreightWaves: Over 284 out-of-service violations uncovered in latest truck enforcement blitzes](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/over-284-out-of-service-violations-uncovered-in-latest-truck-enforcement-blitzes)

## Big Players Signal Cost Relief

- J.B. Hunt told investors cost pressures are easing and it is winning new dedicated business.
- J.B. Hunt said BNSF's rail outage had minimal impact on its volumes.
- Amazon's 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge expands to bulk storage and replenishment transportation fees starting in February.
