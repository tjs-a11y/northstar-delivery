# Diesel slides to $5.937 while pre-Thanksgiving freight tightens and LA-Long Beach avoids a strike

*Sunday, November 22, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Fuel relief continues, tender rejections may climb into the holiday, a port strike threat eases, and broker liability law keeps shifting.

**Diesel fell 19.0 cents to $5.937 a gallon just as early data points to a tightening market ahead of Thanksgiving. Lower fuel and firmer demand could widen margins this week.**

## Diesel keeps sliding

- $5.937 per gallon: the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-16.
- Down 19.0 cents (3.1%) from the prior week, a meaningful cut to per-mile fuel costs.
- Down 33.9 cents over four weeks, so check whether fuel surcharge tables are lagging the drop.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-16](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Holiday capacity is tightening

- Tender rejections could top 14% before Thanksgiving, according to a SONAR-based outlook, as low inventories spur last-minute shipping.
- That forecast points to stronger spot leverage for carriers with open trucks during holiday week.
- Backup carriers are charging shippers more in tight markets, so contingency freight should not be priced cheap.

Source: [FreightWaves: Trucking Rejection Rates: Why Holiday Shipping Gets SPICY](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-rejection-rates-why-holiday-shipping-gets-spicy) · [FreightWaves: Shippers’ backup plan is getting more expensive](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/shippers-backup-plan-is-getting-more-expensive)

## Port strike threat eases

- A tentative union deal averts an immediate strike at the Port of LA-Long Beach, the nation's busiest container complex.
- The accord follows more than a year of bargaining, including federally mediated talks.
- Drayage and Southern California lanes should avoid a sudden holiday shutdown, though the deal is not yet final.

Source: [FreightWaves: Tentative union deal averts strike at Port of LA-Long Beach](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/tentative-union-deal-averts-strike-at-port-of-la-long-beach)

## Broker liability case moves forward

- J.B. Hunt's brokerage won one ruling and lost another in Arizona federal court on Thursday.
- The case is becoming a key test of broker liability after the Montgomery vs. Caribe Transport II decision.
- Expect brokers to scrutinize carrier vetting more closely as these rulings shape their legal exposure.

Source: [FreightWaves: J.B. Hunt broker liability case proceeding after split decision](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/j-b-hunt-broker-liability-case-proceeding-after-split-decision)

## Mexico shifts on trucking safety

- 29 agreements on highway security were reached by Mexican trucking groups and authorities after a nationwide strike.
- Mexico also added digital compliance requirements for carriers seeking voluntary safety certification.
- Cross-border carriers and their Mexican partners should watch for changing paperwork and security conditions.

Source: [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: Truckers secure 29 agreements on highway safety after nationwide strike](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-truckers-secure-29-agreements-on-highway-safety-after-nationwide-strike) · [FreightWaves: Mexico tightens trucking oversight with new digital compliance rules](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/mexico-tightens-trucking-oversight-with-new-digital-compliance-rules)
