# Diesel eases to $6.292 as English-proficiency enforcement sidelines 33,000 drivers

*Thursday, November 5, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel fell 6.0 cents last week, roadside blitzes widen, Class 8 orders drop 36%, and the LA/Long Beach strike threat is pushed back again.

**Diesel slipped 6.0 cents to $6.292 a gallon but remains up 9.3 cents over four weeks. Meanwhile, DOT says 33,000 drivers have been put out of service over English-language deficiencies.**

## Diesel Takes a Small Breather

- $6.292 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-02.
- Down 6.0 cents (0.9%) week over week, a modest break on fuel spend.
- Up 9.3 cents over four weeks, so check that fuel surcharges still cover your real costs.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-02](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Roadside Enforcement Keeps Tightening

- 33,000 drivers have been placed out of service over English-language deficiencies, according to DOT.
- 283 violations turned up in recent CDL blitzes across Virginia and Arkansas.
- Small fleets should confirm every driver can handle roadside communication before dispatching through blitz states.

Source: [FreightWaves: 33,000 truckers sidelined over violations as highway blitzes hit Arkansas, Virginia](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/33000-truckers-sidelined-over-violations-as-highway-blitzes-hit-arkansas-virginia)

## Class 8 Orders Fall Sharply

- 36% year-over-year drop in North American Class 8 orders in October, per FTR.
- Regulatory uncertainty at EPA, equipment costs and tighter budgets are holding fleets back.
- Fewer new trucks entering service could help tighten capacity over time for carriers keeping current equipment.

Source: [FreightWaves: Class 8 truck orders plunge 36% as EPA uncertainty rattles fleets](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/class-8-truck-orders-plunge-36-as-epa-uncertainty-rattles-fleets)

## LA/Long Beach Strike Threat Delayed

- A possible strike at the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach has been delayed again.
- Employers and a longshore union local are still in contract talks.
- Drayage and Southern California carriers should watch for disruptions if negotiations break down.

Source: [FreightWaves: Port strike delayed again at Los Angeles, Long Beach ports](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/port-strike-delayed-again-at-los-angeles-long-beach-ports)

## Congress Shake-Up Hits Trucking Policy

- Election results will reshape the House Transportation & Infrastructure committee's membership.
- Several current committee members will not return, affecting the highway bill and trucking regulations ahead.

Source: [FreightWaves: Transportation committee in House set for overhaul post-election](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/transportation-committee-in-house-set-for-overhaul-post-election)
