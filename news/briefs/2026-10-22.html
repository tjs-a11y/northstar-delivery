Diesel climbs 9.7 cents as Knight-Swift targets double-digit contract rate gains

Diesel rose to $6.276, a big carrier signals rate hikes into bid season, railroads post strong results, and LA-Long Beach dwell times climb.

Diesel snaps back up

  • $6.276 per gallon: U.S. average retail diesel for the week of 2026-10-19, per EIA.
  • Up 9.7 cents (1.6%) week over week, so fuel cost per mile just rose.
  • Still down 25.3 cents over four weeks, but last week's jump erased part of that relief.
  • Make sure surcharge tables reflect the latest weekly average before booking freight.

Source: EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-19

Knight-Swift pushes for higher rates

  • Double-digit increases: Knight-Swift is targeting them as its new bid season starts next month.
  • Big carriers chasing contract hikes can lift the rate floor small fleets negotiate against.
  • Knight-Swift beat Q3 expectations, with a fourth-quarter outlook in line with consensus.
  • Ryder improved nearly every third-quarter metric and raised its full-year 2026 outlook.

Source: FreightWaves: Knight-Swift again eyes big rate increases as new bid season nears · FreightWaves: First look: Knight-Swift’s Q3 beats expectations · FreightWaves: First look: almost all strong numbers in Ryder’s third quarter earnings

Rail volumes and profits rise

  • Record revenue: Norfolk Southern's third-quarter earnings rose on higher volume and fuel surcharges.
  • CSX Q3 profit also climbed on more freight and higher fuel surcharge revenue.
  • Stronger rail volumes mean more intermodal competition on long-haul lanes.

Source: FreightWaves: First look: Norfolk Southern earnings · FreightWaves: CSX profit jumps on volume and revenue growth

Port delays and border capacity

  • Record September volumes pushed up cargo dwell times at San Pedro Bay Ports for truck and rail.
  • Longer dwell means more waiting at LA and Long Beach, so price drayage detention accordingly.
  • $90 million: DSV opened a Laredo facility combining cross-border trucking, warehousing and customs.
  • A court challenge targets 50% tariffs on Canada, leaving the cross-border freight outlook uncertain.

Source: Supply Chain Dive: Extended peak season drives high dwell times at San Pedro Bay Ports · FreightWaves: DSV expands US-Mexico freight network with $90M Laredo facility · Supply Chain Dive: Trump’s 50% Canada tariffs challenged in court

Freight shifts to watch

  • Nestlé will close an Ohio factory by end of 2027, moving production to South Carolina.
  • That move could reroute food freight from Ohio lanes toward the Southeast.
  • Primo Brands opened a new Arkansas plant to boost Mountain Valley sparkling water output.
  • Tesla says its Class 8 electric Semi entered volume production ahead of a 2027 expansion.

Source: Supply Chain Dive: Nestlé to close Ohio factory by end of 2027 · Supply Chain Dive: Primo Brands opens first new factory post merger · FreightWaves: Tesla Semi enters volume production as 2027 rollout approaches
