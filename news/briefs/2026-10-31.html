Diesel Jumps 7.6 Cents to $6.352 as Covenant CEO Eyes Double-Digit 2027 Rate Hikes

Diesel climbs to $6.352, Covenant forecasts double-digit contract hikes, Arkansas blitz targets English proficiency, and Mexico truckers pause their strike.

Diesel Climbs Back Up

  • $6.352 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-10-26.
  • Up 7.6 cents (1.2%) week over week, a sharp jump that hits per-mile costs immediately.
  • Still down 3.0 cents over four weeks, so the recent trend remains mixed.
  • Check that surcharge tables on spot and contract loads reflect the latest weekly price.

Source: EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-26

Covenant Sees Higher 2027 Contract Rates

  • Double-digit truckload contract rate increases in 2027 are expected by Covenant Logistics CEO David Parker.
  • That would mark another year of substantial gains, a sign big carriers see capacity staying tight.
  • Small carriers negotiating lanes now may have room to push for better rates.

Source: FreightWaves: Covenant Logistics CEO expects double-digit truckload rate hikes in 2027

Arkansas Blitz Targets Driver Compliance

  • 183 English-language proficiency violations were written in a 48-hour Arkansas enforcement blitz.
  • 44 criminal arrests and 74 immigration violations also resulted from the two-day operation.
  • Expect roadside inspections to keep focusing on language proficiency; make sure drivers are prepared.

Source: FreightWaves: Arkansas truck blitz nets 183 English violations, 44 arrests in 48 hours

Mexico Strike on Hold

  • Mexican trucking leaders suspended their nationwide strike, but only temporarily.
  • They want measurable improvements in highway security from negotiations over deadly cargo crime.
  • Cross-border carriers should watch for renewed disruption if talks stall.

Source: FreightWaves: Mexico truckers halt protests, demand action on deadly highway crime

Rail, Rates and Big Shippers

  • Schneider struck a deal to use Norfolk Southern's eastern rail network and raised its outlook despite a Q3 miss.
  • The Fed held rates steady despite accelerating inflation; two members wanted a 0.25-point hike, keeping equipment financing costly.
  • Walmart plans a $400M, 744,000-plus-square-foot distribution center in Cibolo, Texas, breaking ground in February.

Source: FreightWaves: Schneider partners with Norfolk Southern; raises outlook despite Q3 miss · Supply Chain Dive: Fed holds main interest rate steady despite accelerating inflation · Supply Chain Dive: Walmart plans $400M Texas distribution center
