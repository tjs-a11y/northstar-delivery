# Court challenge hits 50% Canada tariffs as Heartland posts a real Q3 profit

*Wednesday, October 21, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

A lawsuit targets Canada tariffs, Heartland Express turns a profit, nearly $1M in cards vanish from a truck, and LTL and rail networks shift.

**A lawsuit at the Court of International Trade challenges the 50% tariffs on Canada. Cross-border carriers should watch the case closely.**

## Canada Tariffs Face Legal Test

- 50% tariffs on Canada are now challenged in a suit filed Tuesday at the Court of International Trade.
- The suit claims the president overstepped by using a statute nearly 100 years old to impose them.
- Cross-border carriers should treat the case as a key watch item for northbound and southbound lane volumes.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Trump’s 50% Canada tariffs challenged in court](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/trumps-50-canada-tariffs-challenged-in-court/833495/)

## Heartland Express Turns the Corner

- Q3 brought Heartland its first net profit since 2023 that did not lean on big equipment or terminal sales.
- A large truckload carrier earning from operations is one more data point small fleets can weigh when reading the market.

Source: [FreightWaves: Heartland Express sees earnings turnaround in Q3](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/heartland-express-sees-earnings-turnaround-in-q3)

## Nearly $1M Cargo Theft in California

- Nearly $1 million in trading cards disappeared from a FedEx truck outside a California grading facility.
- The driver found a cargo-door lock missing, and detectives are seeking surveillance footage.
- High-value, easy-to-resell loads remain prime targets, so check locks and seals at every stop.

Source: [FreightWaves: Nearly $1M in Pokémon and sports cards vanish from FedEx truck, driver discovers missing lock](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/nearly-1m-in-pokemon-and-sports-cards-vanish-from-fedex-truck-driver-discovers-missing-lock)

## Network Moves in LTL and Rail

- A. Duie Pyle is adding a Cincinnati-area LTL service center and a Camp Hill, Pennsylvania warehouse.
- That expansion could mean more regional LTL competition and capacity in Ohio and Pennsylvania.
- CSX cut more than 100 management positions while restructuring its engineering department.
