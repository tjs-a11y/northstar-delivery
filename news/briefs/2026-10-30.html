Diesel rises 7.6 cents, Schneider picks Norfolk Southern over CSX, Mexico truckers pause strike, and the Fed holds rates.

Diesel jumps 7.6 cents

  • $6.352 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-10-26.
  • Up 7.6 cents (1.2%) week over week, so check that fuel surcharges are keeping pace.
  • Still down 3.0 cents over four weeks, but the one-week spike hits margins right away.

Source: EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-26

Schneider swaps CSX for Norfolk Southern

  • Schneider is making Norfolk Southern its primary eastern rail provider, replacing CSX.
  • The move comes as Norfolk Southern pursues a merger with Union Pacific, which could reshape eastern intermodal lanes.
  • Schneider raised its outlook despite a slight Q3 miss, a sign that big fleets see demand holding.

Source: FreightWaves: Schneider partners with Norfolk Southern; raises outlook despite Q3 miss · FreightWaves: Schneider to shift eastern rail business to Norfolk Southern from CSX

Mexico truckers pause national strike

  • Mexican trucking leaders have temporarily suspended their nationwide strike over deadly highway crime.
  • They say talks must deliver measurable security gains, so cross-border disruptions could return.

Source: FreightWaves: Mexico truckers halt protests, demand action on deadly highway crime

Shipper and LTL signals

  • 11% was the jump in Amazon's Q3 shipping costs, driven by higher fuel and linehaul expenses.
  • FedEx Freight says LTL volumes are stabilizing and expects Q4 margins to improve.
  • FedEx expects a strong peak season, a possible boost for spot demand.
  • Covenant's Q3 revenue rose 19.2% to $353.9 million, led by growth in managed freight.

Source: FreightWaves: Amazon battles freight inflation as shipping costs jump 11% in Q3 · FreightWaves: FedEx Freight sees volumes stabilize, eyes Q4 margin improvement · FreightWaves: FedEx cashes in on premium logistics services, fuel surcharges · FreightWaves: First look: Covenant Logistics’ Q3 freight revenue surges 68%

Rates, rail and new freight nodes

  • The Fed held its main rate steady despite accelerating inflation, so equipment financing costs stay put for now.
  • 26,000 containers a year are targeted to move off the road by Georgia's new inland rail hub, which could cut local drayage.
  • $400M Walmart distribution center in Cibolo, Texas, is set to break ground this February.

Source: Supply Chain Dive: Fed holds main interest rate steady despite accelerating inflation · FreightWaves: New Georgia hub aims to take 26,000 containers off the road · Supply Chain Dive: Walmart plans $400M Texas distribution center
