## Diesel Keeps Sliding

- $5.937 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-16.
- Down 19.0 cents (3.1%) from the prior week, a meaningful cut to per-mile fuel costs.
- Down 33.9 cents over four weeks, so fuel surcharge tables may now lag what you actually pay.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-16](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## DOL Contractor Rule Stays Put

- The Labor Department's final independent contractor rule barely changed from its February proposal.
- Trucking groups welcomed the rule, which replaces the Biden-era version on how contractors are classified.
- Lease-on owner-operators and carriers using them should review agreements against the final text.

Source: [FreightWaves: DOL sticks with independent contractor proposal, trucking applauds](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/dol-sticks-with-independent-contractor-proposal-trucking-applauds)

## Cargo Theft Cases Pile Up

- 30 months in federal prison for a trucker tied to a $7.2 million Southern California cargo theft conspiracy.
- Thieves used real freight assignments and trucking companies as cover, so vet partners and loads carefully.
- 14 people charged in a separate $22.5 million ring that stopped moving freight trains to steal goods.
- Stolen Nike sneakers, Milwaukee tools and electronics were moved through storage sites into resale markets.

Source: [FreightWaves: Trucker sentenced in $7.2 million cargo theft scheme involving legitimate freight contracts](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucker-sentenced-in-7-2-million-cargo-theft-scheme-involving-legitimate-freight-contracts) · [FreightWaves: 14 charged in $22.5 million cargo theft ring that stopped moving freight trains](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/14-charged-in-22-5-million-cargo-theft-ring-that-stopped-moving-freight-trains)

## Holiday Capacity Could Tighten

- Tender rejection rates could top 14% ahead of Thanksgiving, according to FreightWaves SONAR data.
- Low retail inventories may drive last-minute shipping, which can lift spot opportunities for available trucks.
- Possible port strikes could further squeeze capacity in some markets.

Source: [FreightWaves: Trucking Rejection Rates: Why Holiday Shipping Gets SPICY](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-rejection-rates-why-holiday-shipping-gets-spicy)

## Rail Gains and Merger Fight

- 7.3% year-over-year rise in U.S. rail traffic, with intermodal up 10.7%, adds competition for long-haul freight.
- Grain carloads declined in the same week.
- Union Pacific and Norfolk Southern are fighting a request to end review of their coast-to-coast merger, drawing labor support and shipper concerns.

Source: [FreightWaves: US rail traffic rises 7.3% as intermodal gains 10.7% in latest week](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/us-rail-traffic-rises-7-3-as-intermodal-gains-10-7-in-latest-week) · [FreightWaves: Union Pacific–Norfolk Southern merger draws labor support, shipper concerns](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/union-pacific-norfolk-southern-merger-draws-labor-support-shipper-concerns)
