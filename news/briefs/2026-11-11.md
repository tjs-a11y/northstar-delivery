# Diesel slides 16.5 cents as a tighter peak squeezes old shipper bid playbooks

*Wednesday, November 11, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel averages $6.127, rigid shipper contracts strain in a firmer market, intermodal sets a record and a Texas carrier faces federal charges.

**The national diesel average dropped 16.5 cents to $6.127 per gallon, a welcome break on fuel costs, though oil markets remain volatile.**

## Diesel drops sharply this week

- $6.127 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-09.
- Down 16.5 cents (2.6%) from the prior week, easing per-mile fuel costs for small carriers.
- Down 5.2 cents over four weeks, so most of the relief landed in just the last week.
- Oil markets remain volatile on geopolitical and refining risks, so keep fuel surcharge terms in place.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-09](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/) · [FreightWaves: Diesel Price Drop: Why Oil Markets Are Tumbling](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/diesel-price-drop-why-oil-markets-are-tumbling)

## Rigid shipper contracts strain at peak

- Long rate locks, steep penalties and little flexibility have been some shippers' standard bid strategy from 2022 through 2025.
- A brokerage leader at A. Duie Pyle says that approach is breaking down as the market tightens.
- Shippers stuck with stale contract rates may lean on spot capacity, opening better-paying loads for small carriers.

Source: [FreightWaves: Shippers running 2022-era bid playbooks face a tighter peak](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/a-duie-pyle-peak-season-shipper-bids)

## Intermodal hits record in October

- Intermodal set a record in October, according to the AAR.
- Rail volumes rose across much of the freight economy, a sign of continued demand.
- More intermodal means more drayage work near ramps but competition for some long-haul dry van lanes.

Source: [FreightWaves: US rail traffic hits intermodal record as October freight gains signal economic resilience, says AAR](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/us-rail-traffic-hits-intermodal-record-as-october-freight-gains-signal-economic-resilience-says-aar)

## Texas owner indicted over 57 drivers

- 57 unauthorized B-1 visa drivers were allegedly used by a Laredo trucking company owner to haul domestic freight.
- The owner faces federal charges, showing enforcement against illegal domestic hauling is active.
- Carriers playing by the rules lose loads to operators cutting corners, so this crackdown matters to rates.

Source: [FreightWaves: Texas trucking owner indicted in scheme involving 57 unauthorized drivers](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/texas-trucking-owner-indicted-in-scheme-involving-57-unauthorized-drivers)

## Ocean trade: fees delayed, rates ease

- Two more months: the U.S. postponed punitive fees on China-linked ships until January.
- Transpacific ocean rates eased slightly as peak demand at U.S. ports slows.
- Rates stay elevated year over year, so expect import drayage volumes to cool rather than collapse.

Source: [FreightWaves: US postpones ‘million-dollar’ China ship tax until January](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/us-postpones-million-dollar-china-ship-tax-until-january) · [Supply Chain Dive: Transpacific ocean rates ease slightly as peak winds down](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/transpacific-ocean-rates-ease-slightly-as-peak-winds-down/874262/)
