## Diesel Bounces Back Up

- $6.276 per gallon is the U.S. average diesel price for the week of 2026-10-19, per EIA.
- Up 9.7 cents (1.6%) week over week, so check that fuel surcharges are keeping pace on every load.
- Still down 25.3 cents over four weeks, but last week's jump shows relief is not locked in.
- Reefer operators burn fuel in both truck and trailer unit, so they feel each increase twice.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-10-19](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Contract Rate Gains Hold Steady

- Above 10% is where truckload contract rate increases keep landing, according to a new SONAR index.
- Carriers are winning in one cycle what would normally take several years of increases.
- Small fleets renewing lanes now have leverage to push for higher contract rates.

Source: [FreightWaves: Truckload contract rate increases riding steady above 10%](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/truckload-contract-rate-increases-riding-steady-above-10)

## CSX Courts Truckload Shippers

- CSX is reviewing shippers' truckload files to offer service and cost comparisons for intermodal.
- The railroad sees a chance to pull freight from a battered trucking market onto rail.
- Long-haul dry van carriers should watch for shippers testing rail on longer lanes.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: CSX sees rail conversion opportunities from battered trucking market](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/csx-sees-rail-conversion-opportunities-from-battered-trucking-market/833858/)

## Rate Hikes and Cross-Border Moves

- 5.9% general rate increase from Roadrunner takes effect Nov. 2, alongside a new Canada-to-U.S. LTL service.
- 5.9% average rate increase from UPS starts Dec. 21, matching FedEx's coming bump.
- Oct. 30: plywood and certain packaging materials will no longer face the 50% Canada tariff, which could lift those flows.
- $641M in U.S. trucking and warehousing activity comes from avocado imports out of Mexico.

Source: [FreightWaves: Roadrunner announces Canada-outbound service](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/roadrunner-announces-canada-outbound-service) · [Supply Chain Dive: UPS plans 5.9% rate increase on Dec. 21](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/ups-plans-59-rate-increase-on-dec-21/872751/) · [Supply Chain Dive: Trump tweaks Canada tariffs, removing certain goods while adding others](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/trump-tweaks-canada-tariffs-removing-certain-goods-while-adding-others/833895/) · [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: Avocado imports generate $641M for US trucking, warehousing](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-avocado-imports-generate-641m-for-us-trucking-warehousing)
