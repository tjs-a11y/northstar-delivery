# Diesel Falls to $6.127 While October Truckload Rates Surge and Imports Set a Record

*Monday, November 16, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Cheaper fuel meets firmer rates, record container imports, rising AI cargo theft and a fresh tariff threat on Mexico freight.

**Diesel dropped 16.5 cents to $6.127 a gallon just as truckload rates posted significant October gains. That widens margins for carriers hauling in a tightening market.**

## Diesel Takes a Big Weekly Drop

- $6.127 per gallon is the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-09.
- Down 16.5 cents (2.6%) from the prior week, a meaningful break on fuel costs per load.
- Down 5.2 cents over four weeks, so the relief is recent rather than a long trend.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-09](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Rates Heat Up Before the Holidays

- Truckload rates posted significant gains in October, per Cass Information Systems.
- Shipment counts grew for a third consecutive month, a sign that demand is firming under the rate gains.
- Spot rates are climbing ahead of the holidays earlier than usual, which creates room to push for better linehaul pay.

Source: [FreightWaves: October TL rates surge as shipment counts extend positive streak](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/october-tl-rates-surge-as-shipment-counts-extend-positive-streak) · [FreightWaves: Spot rates jump ahead of holidays](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/spot-rates-jump-ahead-of-holidays)

## Record Imports Clog Ports and Rail

- 2.67 million TEUs of U.S. container imports in October set a monthly record, per Descartes.
- Imports rose 16% year over year and topped the prior record from May 2022, which means more freight for drayage and outbound loads.
- Port delays are rising, so expect longer waits at terminals.
- At the Port of Los Angeles, containers waiting for rail are sitting over nine days, which could shift more volume to trucks.

Source: [FreightWaves: U.S. container imports hit record 2.67 million TEUs in October as port delays rise](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/u-s-container-imports-hit-record-2-67-million-teus-in-october-as-port-delays-rise) · [Supply Chain Dive: Port of Los Angeles grapples with elevated rail dwell times](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/port-of-los-angeles-grapples-with-elevated-rail-dwell-times/874553/)

## AI Cargo Theft Tops $150 Million

- More than $150 million in AI equipment was stolen from June 2025 to June 2026, per Overhaul.
- 92% of reported incidents involved fraudulent carrier identities or falsified shipping documents.
- Carriers should expect brokers to tighten identity checks on high-value tech loads.

Source: [FreightWaves: AI cargo thefts top $150 million from June 2025 to June 2026](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ai-cargo-thefts-top-150-million-from-june-2025-to-june-2026)

## Mexico Freight Faces Tariff Threat

- The president threatened to double tariffs on Mexico unless it halts migrant caravans, effective immediately if triggered.
- $75.4B in truck freight moved between the U.S. and Mexico in September, so cross-border haulers have a lot at stake.
- Texas cross-border traffic rose by 400 trucks under a Saturday pilot program.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Trump threatens to double Mexico tariffs over migrant caravans](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/trump-threatens-to-double-mexico-tariffs-over-migrant-caravans/874534/) · [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: Texas cross-border traffic rises by 400 trucks through Saturday pilot](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-texas-cross-border-traffic-rises-by-400-trucks-through-saturday-pilot)
