# Freight Demand Jumps Nearly 5% as Spot Rates Keep Climbing; Diesel Dips to $6.292

*Monday, November 9, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Tighter truckload capacity, a small diesel dip, a lame-duck Congress clock and an $11.4 million theft lead today's brief.

**Freight demand rose nearly 5% week over week, tender rejections are near 13%, and spot rates are still climbing.**

## Demand up, capacity tightening

- Nearly 5%: weekly freight demand gain, giving carriers more loads to choose from.
- Near 13%: tender rejection rate, meaning more contract freight is spilling into the spot market.
- Van contract and spot rates are both rising, a good moment to push for stronger linehaul offers.
- Flatbed and LTL conditions were also tracked, with spot markets strengthening in some areas.

Source: [FreightWaves: Freight Demand Jumps 5% as Spot Rates Climb](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freight-demand-jumps-5-as-spot-rates-climb)

## Diesel slips, still up for month

- $6.292 per gallon: U.S. average retail diesel for the week of 2026-11-02.
- Down 6.0 cents (0.9%) from the prior week, a small break at the pump.
- Up 9.3 cents over four weeks, so fuel surcharges still matter in rate talks.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-02](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

## Congress clock runs out Jan. 3

- Noon, Jan. 3, 2027: any trucking bill not passed by both chambers and sent to the president dies.
- Election results shifted the political math, with Democrats winning control, changing odds for pending trucking legislation.
- Unfinished bills would restart next Congress with new numbers and new committee chairs.

Source: [FreightWaves: What trucking can still get from a lame-duck Congress before Jan. 3](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/what-trucking-can-still-get-from-a-lame-duck-congress-before-jan-3)

## $11.4M load vanishes in transit

- $11.4 million: reported value of NVIDIA hardware that disappeared between California and Texas.
- Oakland police are investigating it as grand theft; the carrier's identity remains unclear.
- High-value electronics remain a top theft target, so vetting and load security deserve extra attention.

Source: [FreightWaves: $11.4 million in NVIDIA hardware vanishes during shipment from California to Texas](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/11-4-million-in-nvidia-hardware-vanishes-during-shipment-from-california-to-texas)

## West Coast ports: strike delayed

- LA-Long Beach terminal watchmen and employers pushed back a strike deadline as contract talks continue.
- A walkout could still disrupt drayage and container flow out of the nation's busiest port complex.
- 343%: rise in Asia-U.S. West Coast container spot rates since the Iran war began, per Xeneta.

Source: [FreightWaves: Strike at Los Angeles-Long Beach port delayed as union contract talks continue](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/strike-at-los-angeles-long-beach-port-delayed-as-union-contract-talks-continue) · [FreightWaves: Asia-U.S. West Coast ocean container spot rates up 343% since start of Iran war, says Xeneta](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/asia-u-s-west-coast-ocean-container-spot-rates-up-343-since-start-of-iran-war-says-xeneta)
