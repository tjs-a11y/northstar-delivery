FMCSA Revokes 133 ELDs as Mexico Tariff Threat Clouds Cross-Border Freight

Regulators pull 133 logging devices, Trump threatens to double Mexico tariffs, and LA port volume and rail delays climb together.

FMCSA removed 133 electronic logging devices from its approved registry, citing fraud and compliance failures. Carriers should confirm their ELD is still listed.

133 ELDs Pulled From Registry

  • 133 devices: FMCSA struck them from its approved ELD list in a fraud crackdown.
  • Regulators cited widespread compliance failures and evidence of fraud behind the removals.
  • Running a revoked device can put your logs and roadside inspections at risk, so verify your unit's status now.

Source: FreightWaves: FMCSA revokes 133 electronic logging devices in fraud crackdown

Mexico Tariffs Could Double

  • Doubled tariffs: Trump threatened them on Mexican imports unless Mexico stops migrant caravans.
  • Effective immediately: the president said the higher levies would hit if Mexico fails to act.
  • Cross-border and border-lane carriers could see freight volumes swing quickly if the threat is carried out.

Source: Supply Chain Dive: Trump threatens to double Mexico tariffs over migrant caravans · FreightWaves: Trump threatens to double Mexico tariffs over alleged migrant influx

LA Booms While Rail Backs Up

  • Over 1 million TEUs: Los Angeles topped that mark for a second straight month in a record October.
  • 5%: October cargo ran ahead of 2025, keeping Southern California drayage demand strong.
  • Over nine days: rail-bound containers are sitting idle at the port, per Executive Director Gene Seroka.
  • Long rail dwell can push more boxes toward trucks and tie up chassis and terminal time.

Source: FreightWaves: Record October as Los Angeles tops 1 Million TEUs for second straight month · Supply Chain Dive: Port of Los Angeles grapples with elevated rail dwell times

Dash Cams and Nuclear Verdicts

  • $604M: a recent verdict has defense attorneys warning fleets about gaps in AI-only dash cams.
  • Plaintiff lawyers hunt for systemic failure, not just crash facts, says defense attorney Doug Marcello.
  • Risky driving a camera recorded but nobody reviewed can become evidence against a small fleet.

Source: FreightWaves: After $604M verdict, fleets face AI camera blind spots

Trade Policy Gets Murkier

  • Both chambers: Democrats' midterm wins add uncertainty to the trade outlook.
  • Three bills in Congress would sharply limit presidential tariff power if they become law.
  • Trans-Pacific ocean rates stay elevated as imports ease from a near-record peak, a signal for port-lane demand.

Source: FreightWaves: US midterm shift adds trade uncertainty as trans-Pacific ocean freight rates stay elevated · Supply Chain Dive: Bipartisan push possible to curb Trump tariffs
