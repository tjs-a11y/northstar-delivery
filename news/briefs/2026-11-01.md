# Covenant CEO Expects Double-Digit 2027 Contract Hikes as Roadside Enforcement Tightens

*Sunday, November 1, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Truckload contract rates may climb again in 2027, Arkansas runs a major enforcement blitz, and border trucking faces new pressures.

**Covenant Logistics' CEO expects double-digit truckload contract rate increases in 2027, a sign shippers may face another year of rising costs.**

## Contract Rates Seen Climbing in 2027

- Double-digit truckload contract hikes are expected in 2027, per Covenant Logistics CEO David Parker.
- That would follow another year of substantial contract increases, giving small carriers leverage in upcoming rate talks.
- Freight cycles since 2000 have behaved more like random shocks than clockwork, so plan for surprises.

Source: [FreightWaves: Covenant Logistics CEO expects double-digit truckload rate hikes in 2027](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/covenant-logistics-ceo-expects-double-digit-truckload-rate-hikes-in-2027) · [FreightWaves: Freight cycles are anything but regular](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freight-cycles-are-anything-but-regular)

## Arkansas Blitz Hits Drivers Hard

- 183 English-language proficiency violations were written during a two-day Arkansas enforcement operation.
- 44 criminal arrests and 74 immigration violations also resulted within 48 hours.
- Expect English proficiency checks at roadside stops; make sure every driver can handle inspector questions.

Source: [FreightWaves: Arkansas truck blitz nets 183 English violations, 44 arrests in 48 hours](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/arkansas-truck-blitz-nets-183-english-violations-44-arrests-in-48-hours)

## Border Trucking Under Pressure

- Cabotage enforcement is emerging as a threat to cross-border trucking operations.
- 50% toll cut on the Colombia Bridge from Nuevo León aims to draw more cross-border truck traffic.
- Mexican truckers paused their nationwide strike but want measurable highway security gains from negotiations.

Source: [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: Cabotage enforcements threatening cross-border trucking](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-cabotage-enforcements-threatening-cross-border-trucking) · [FreightWaves: Mexico truckers halt protests, demand action on deadly highway crime](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/mexico-truckers-halt-protests-demand-action-on-deadly-highway-crime)

## New Walmart DC Near San Antonio

- $400M distribution center planned in Cibolo, Texas, spanning more than 744,000 square feet.
- 425 jobs expected, with groundbreaking slated for February, pointing to future regional freight demand.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: Walmart plans $400M Texas distribution center](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/walmart-plans-400m-texas-distribution-center/873218/) · [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: Cabotage enforcements threatening cross-border trucking](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-cabotage-enforcements-threatening-cross-border-trucking)

## Fed Holds; Autonomous Trucks Lag

- The Fed kept its main rate steady despite accelerating inflation, so equipment financing costs stay elevated.
- Two Fed policymakers dissented in favor of a 0.25 percentage point hike.
- Aurora posted a $257 million operating loss on $3 million in revenue and cut its full-year forecast.
- Aurora still says it will produce more than 1000 trucks in 2027.
