# Diesel eases 6 cents as FMCSA enforcement push looks set to stick

*Sunday, November 8, 2026 · NorthStar Freight Brief*

Diesel slips week over week, an industry CEO says the CDL crackdown will last, and a fraud report flags hacked communications in load theft.

**Diesel averaged $6.292 per gallon, down 6.0 cents on the week but still up 9.3 cents over four weeks. Expect fuel to keep driving rates and surcharges.**

## Diesel Pulls Back, Still Elevated

- $6.292 per gallon was the U.S. average retail diesel price for the week of 2026-11-02, per EIA.
- Down 6.0 cents (0.9%) from the prior week, a small break on fuel costs.
- Up 9.3 cents over four weeks, so check that fuel surcharges still cover your real per-mile cost.
- Fuel surcharges are driving most of this fall's intermodal contract increases, keeping rail pricing tied to diesel.

Source: [EIA: Weekly U.S. diesel price, week of 2026-11-02](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/) · [FreightWaves: Intermodal rates coming in hot this fall; mostly fuel driven](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/intermodal-rates-coming-in-hot-this-fall-mostly-fuel-driven)

## FMCSA Crackdown Expected to Stick

- FreightWaves CEO Craig Fuller says big carriers are keeping FMCSA's enforcement push alive.
- He argues non-domiciled CDL holders were probably the main reason the freight downturn dragged on.
- His view: enforcement will outlast any political change in Washington, so keep compliance files tight.

Source: [FreightWaves: Big carriers keep FMCSA’s crackdown alive, Fuller says](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/fuller-big-carriers-fmcsa-crackdown-permanent)

## Fraud: Hacked Comms Behind Theft

- 40% of stolen loads were linked to compromised communications, according to a Highway fraud report.
- Fraudulent calls surged 90%, so verify callers before acting on load or payment changes.
- Carrier ownership reports more than doubled, pointing to more disputes over who controls an authority.

Source: [FreightWaves: Compromised communications linked to 40% of stolen loads in Highway fraud report](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/compromised-communications-linked-to-40-of-stolen-loads-in-highway-fraud-report)

## Border Lanes: Security and Trade

- 826 National Guard officers will be deployed by Mexico to boost highway security, relevant to cross-border cargo safety.
- 1,000-plus pounds of meth turned up in a produce truck at the Texas border after a canine alert.
- U.S. foreign-trade zones are pushing to fix USMCA tariff obstacles affecting cross-border freight.

Source: [FreightWaves: Borderlands Mexico: US foreign-trade zones push to fix USMCA tariff obstacles](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/borderlands-mexico-us-foreign-trade-zones-push-to-fix-usmca-tariff-obstacles) · [FreightWaves: CBP seizes 1,000-plus pounds of meth in produce truck at Texas border](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/cbp-seizes-1000-plus-pounds-of-meth-in-produce-truck-at-texas-border)

## Rail Shakeups Touch Drayage Work

- Union Pacific's CEO said he'd raise rates on shippers opposing the Norfolk Southern merger; UP called it an offhand comment.
- Schneider National is shifting its intermodal partnership to Norfolk Southern, which could reshape some ramp and drayage volumes.

Source: [Supply Chain Dive: UP CEO’s rate threat adds wrinkle to Norfolk Southern merger process](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/up-ceos-rate-threat-adds-wrinkle-to-norfolk-southern-merger-process/874006/) · [Supply Chain Dive: Schneider National says rail partner shift offers network boost](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/schneider-national-says-rail-partner-shift-offers-network-boost/873847/)
