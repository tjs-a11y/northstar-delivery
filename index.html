Rate Con Checker

The setoff clause on page two

The setupA broker sends a clean-looking rate con for a $2,400 load. Buried in the fine print: a setoff clause that lets them pull money from THIS load to cover a claim on a different one.

Without itYou sign, you deliver clean, and a month later your pay gets short-stopped for someone else's problem — and now you're the one chasing it.

With the toolYou paste the rate con, it flags the setoff in red before you sign, and drafts the email asking them to strike it. Thirty seconds.

Haul Audit

The short-pay hiding behind the factoring fee

The setupYour settlement lands lighter than the rate con said. Between the factoring fee, a reserve and an "adjustment," you can't tell if the broker actually short-paid you or it's just the factor's cut.

Without itIt looks close enough, so you let it go — load after load, a few dollars at a time, money you earned and never got.

With the toolHaul Audit rebuilds the agreed gross, isolates the factoring fees, and shows the broker variance on its own — so a real short-pay has nowhere to hide and you dispute it with the math in hand.

Freight Class + Linear Feet

The reclass bill that eats the margin

The setupYou quote an LTL shipment at the class you guessed, and book the lane. The carrier re-measures at the dock and reclasses it.

Without itA reclass or a "you needed a full truck" surprise lands on the invoice — and the margin you quoted is gone before the freight moves.

With the toolsEnter the real dimensions and weight: get the correct freight class and density, and see exactly how much deck the load takes — so the quote holds and the mode is right the first time.
