Shipper guide

Power only: your trailer, our truck.

Power only means a carrier supplies the tractor and driver, and you supply the trailer. It suits shippers and manufacturers with their own trailers or trailer pools, drop-and-hook programs, and moving empty trailers between locations.

Get A Freight Quote

When power only makes sense

  • You keep preloaded trailers at your dock (drop-and-hook), so trucks hook up and leave without waiting.
  • You need to reposition empty or loaded trailers between plants, warehouses or yards.
  • Your own fleet is short on drivers or tractors during a peak.

What to include in your request

  • Trailer type and length, and whether it is loaded or empty.
  • Trailer condition and paperwork: registration, plates, and inspection status.
  • Pickup and drop locations, and yard or gate procedures.
  • Whether the trailer must come back (round trip) or stays at the destination.
