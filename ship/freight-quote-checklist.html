Shipper guide

The one-page freight quote checklist.

Fill this in before you ask for a quote and your quote will match your load. Print it, keep it at the shipping desk, or share it with your team. Free to use and link to.

Get A Freight Quote

Shipment

  • ☐ Pickup: company, address, ZIP, dock or no dock, hours
  • ☐ Delivery: company, address, ZIP, dock or no dock, hours
  • ☐ Earliest pickup date ____ · Latest delivery date ____
  • ☐ Appointments needed? Pickup ☐ Delivery ☐

Freight

  • ☐ Commodity, in plain words: __________
  • ☐ Total weight ____ lb · heaviest piece ____ lb
  • ☐ Pallets or pieces: count ____ · size ____ · stackable? Y / N  (linear feet calculator · freight class calculator)
  • ☐ Oversize? Length ____ · width ____ · height ____
  • ☐ Hazardous materials? Y / N (if yes, UN number and class)

Equipment

  • ☐ Dry van ☐ Reefer (set point ____ °F, continuous / cycle) ☐ Flatbed ☐ Step deck ☐ RGN ☐ Power only ☐ Other ____
  • ☐ Tarps / straps / chains needed?
  • ☐ Team drivers needed?

Extras

  • ☐ Liftgate ☐ Inside delivery ☐ Lumper at delivery ☐ Driver assist ☐ Extra stops (how many ____)
  • ☐ Free time agreed at each stop: ____ hours
