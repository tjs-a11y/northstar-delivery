Why linear feet decide your rate

A 53-foot trailer holds about 26 to 30 standard pallet positions in two rows. When your freight takes up a big share of that deck, carriers stop pricing it as ordinary LTL and switch to volume LTL or a full truckload rate — often cheaper per pound, but priced completely differently.

The rough thresholds

  • Standard LTL: up to ~6 pallets / ~12 linear feet.
  • Volume LTL: roughly 12–24 linear feet, or more than ~6 pallets.
  • Full truckload: freight filling most of the 53 ft deck, or that can't share space.

Carriers charge for the floor space a pallet blocks even when nothing stacks on top, which is why "linear feet" — not just weight — drives the price. Get the exact number, then request a quote for your lane.
