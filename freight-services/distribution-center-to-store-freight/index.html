Important equipment and safety note

A DC release does not guarantee the store can receive the shipment at the same time; both ends must be coordinated.

Frequently asked questions

What delays DC-to-store freight?

Release issues, appointment mismatch, access restrictions and unloading constraints.

What should the shipper confirm first?

Freight readiness and the store's receiving window.
