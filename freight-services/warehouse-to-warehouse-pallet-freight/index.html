Warehouse-to-Warehouse Pallet Freight

Plan pallet freight between warehouses by confirming pallet count, dimensions, weight, dock access, appointments and shipment documentation.

Warehouse-to-warehouse pallet freight requires matching pallet dimensions and weight to the equipment and both facilities' dock procedures.

Who this equipment is suited for

Businesses transferring palletized stock between distribution or storage locations.

Shipment details to confirm

  • Pallet footprint, height and weight
  • Total pallet count and stackability
  • Dock hours and trailer access
  • Pickup release and receiving appointment

Equipment and dispatch planning

  • Verify pallet dimensions and quantities.
  • Confirm loading and unloading capability at both sites.
  • Record the shipment and delivery receipt.
Important equipment and safety note

Non-standard pallets and restricted docks can affect fit, loading time and price.

Frequently asked questions

What details affect a pallet freight quote?

Pallet size, count, total weight, lane and dock/appointment requirements.

Can pallets be double-stacked?

Only when cargo, packaging, shipper instructions and equipment safely permit it.
