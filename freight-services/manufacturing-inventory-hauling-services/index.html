Important equipment and safety note

Do not treat a production target as a confirmed pickup appointment; actual readiness and capacity must be checked.

Frequently asked questions

What details help plan a plant-to-warehouse move?

Commodity, dimensions, weight, packaging, ready time, dock access and destination schedule.

Can a shipment be scheduled before production is complete?

It can be forecast, but dispatch should wait for a confirmed ready time and capacity.
