Last-Mile Commercial Freight Delivery

Reliable B2B last-mile distribution networks. Licensed transport execution ensuring safe final-mile delivery of heavy retail and commercial cargo.

Commercial last-mile delivery is determined by the final site's access and receiving process as much as by the linehaul distance.

Who this equipment is suited for

Palletized or packaged freight going to a business, retail or receiving location with defined site requirements.

Shipment details to confirm

  • Delivery address and vehicle access restrictions
  • Pallet dimensions, weight and count
  • Receiving hours and appointment rules
  • Dock, liftgate or other unloading method
  • Signed receipt and damage documentation

Equipment and dispatch planning

  • Confirm the delivery site can accept the proposed vehicle.
  • Agree on unloading responsibility and appointment details.
  • Document delivery condition and exceptions.
Important equipment and safety note

Inside delivery, installation, liftgate service and exact delivery windows must not be assumed unless written into the shipment agreement.

Frequently asked questions

What causes a commercial final-mile delay?

Restricted access, missed appointments, unavailable unloading equipment or incomplete receiving instructions.

What proves delivery?

The agreed proof-of-delivery record, such as a signed receipt or approved electronic confirmation.
