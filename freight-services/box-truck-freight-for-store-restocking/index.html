Equipment and dispatch planning

  • Agree on unloading and proof of delivery.
Important equipment and safety note

A store's curb or parking layout may restrict vehicle access; verify before dispatch.

Frequently asked questions

Can a box truck deliver to a store without a dock?

Only if the vehicle, unloading method and site access are suitable and confirmed.

What should be measured?

Pallets, door clearance, cargo height and total weight.
